How HRA exemption is calculated

For many salaried employees under the old income tax regime, exempt HRA is the minimum of three amounts: actual HRA received, rent paid minus 10% of basic salary, and 50% of basic (metro) or 40% of basic (non-metro).

Use this with our income tax calculator to compare new vs old regime when HRA is a large deduction.

How to use the HRA calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Basic salary (annual ₹), HRA received (annual ₹) and Rent paid (annual ₹).
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate exemption. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Does HRA apply in the new tax regime?

Generally no—HRA exemption is not available under the new regime. This tool is for old-regime planning.

Is rent receipt mandatory?

Employers and the tax department may require rent agreements and receipts for claimed HRA—this page only estimates math.

Is this HRA calculator free?

Yes. The HRA calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?
