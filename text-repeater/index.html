How to repeat text

  • Type or paste your text or emoji.
  • Enter how many times to repeat it (up to 10,000).
  • Choose a separator: new line, space, comma, nothing or your own.
  • Optionally number each line, then copy the result or send it on WhatsApp.

Popular uses

Fill birthday wishes and greetings, make emoji walls for chats, create test data for forms and spreadsheets, or generate placeholder lines for design mock-ups. The result updates instantly as you type and is capped at one million characters so your browser stays responsive.

How to use the text repeater

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Text to repeat, Repeat how many times?, Separate with and Result.
  2. Get the result. Press Repeat text. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a limit?

Up to 10,000 repetitions or 1,000,000 characters, whichever comes first.

Why is the WhatsApp button hidden?

Very long messages don’t fit in a share link. Copy the text and paste it into WhatsApp instead.

Please don’t spam

Sending huge repeated messages can get accounts restricted on messaging apps. Use it responsibly.

Is this text repeater free?

Yes. The text repeater is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
