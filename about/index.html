About Tools

A collection of 267+ free calculators and utilities for everyday work—finance, documents, writing, development, and study.

How the tools work

Most tools run as JavaScript in your browser. Text you type and files you open (PDFs, images, Word documents, videos) are processed on your device and are not uploaded to this website.

A few tools need a public service to answer a question—for example the domain age checker queries public RDAP records and the IP lookup asks ipify.org for your public IP address.

Accuracy

Calculators give estimates based on the formulas and rates shown on each page. They are not financial, tax, legal, or medical advice—confirm important numbers with a qualified professional or the official source.

Cost

Every tool is free with no account. The site is supported by advertising; see the privacy policy for how ads work.
