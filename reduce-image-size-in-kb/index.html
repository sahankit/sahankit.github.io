Perfect for online application forms

Exam, job and government portals often limit uploads—for example a photo of 20–50 KB and a signature of 10–20 KB, sometimes at a fixed pixel size such as 200 × 230 px. Choose your image, type the KB limit, optionally enter the required width and height, and press Reduce size.

The tool searches for the highest JPEG quality that fits under your target. If even low quality is too big, it gently scales the image down until it fits—so you get the best-looking photo that meets the limit.

Tips for a clear result

  • Crop to the face or signature first—fewer pixels means better quality at the same size.
  • Use JPG for photos; most forms accept only JPG/JPEG.
  • If a form needs a minimum size too (e.g. 10–20 KB), set the target near the top of the range.
  • Check the pixel size the form asks for and enter it under “exact size in pixels”.

How to use the reduce image size in KB

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.

Frequently asked questions

Will my photo be uploaded?

No. Compression happens in your browser; the photo never leaves your device.

Why can’t it reach my target?

Very small targets combined with a large fixed pixel size may be impossible. Increase the KB target or reduce the width and height.

Can I convert PNG to JPG at the same time?

Yes. Any image you choose is saved as JPG (or WebP), with transparent areas filled white.

Is this reduce image size in KB free?

Yes. The reduce image size in KB is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
