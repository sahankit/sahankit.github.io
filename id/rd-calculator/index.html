How to use the RD calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Setoran bulanan (₹), Tarif (% per tahun) dan Bulan.
  2. Get the result. Press Hitung. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
