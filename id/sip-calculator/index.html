How SIP maturity is estimated

The calculator assumes a fixed monthly investment and a constant annual return, compounded monthly. Real mutual fund returns vary; use this for illustration and planning only.

How to use the SIP calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Monthly SIP (₹) , Years and Expected return (% per year) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
