About the speaking time

Estimate presentation or speech duration from word count and speaking speed (WPM).

Berjalan di browser—tanpa daftar atau instal.

How to use the speaking time

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Word count and Speaking speed (words per minute) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate time . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
