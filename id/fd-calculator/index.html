How to use the FD calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Deposit (₹) , Rate (% p.a.) and Years .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.

When to use the FD calculator

Estimate fixed deposit maturity with quarterly compounding. Illustration only. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
