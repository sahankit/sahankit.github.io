How to use the UK National Insurance

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Annual salary (£) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate NI . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
