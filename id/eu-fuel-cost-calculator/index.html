About the EU fuel trip cost

Estimate fuel cost for a trip from distance, L/100km consumption, and price per litre in euros.

How to use the EU fuel trip cost

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Distance (km) , Consumption (L/100km) and Fuel price (€ / litre) .
  2. Get the result. Press Trip cost . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
