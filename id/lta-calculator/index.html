Estimate how much of your Leave Travel Allowance can be exempt from tax when travel costs and LTA received are known. Uses common salary-tax assumptions.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the LTA calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in LTA diterima dari pemberi kerja (₹ / tahun), Biaya perjalanan aktual yang memenuhi syarat (₹) dan Perjalanan perjalanan diklaim blok ini (maks 2).
  2. Get the result. Press Perkirakan pengecualian. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
