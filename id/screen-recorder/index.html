How to record your screen

  • Tick whether to include tab/system audio and your microphone.
  • Press Mulai merekam and choose the entire screen, a window or a browser tab.
  • Do what you want to capture—a tutorial, presentation, bug report or game clip.
  • Press Berhenti merekam (or “Stop sharing” in the browser bar), preview the video and download it.

Audio, formats and support

Chrome and Edge can capture audio from a browser tab, and on Windows from the whole system; macOS only allows tab audio. Add your microphone to narrate. Videos are saved as WebM (or MP4 where the browser supports it), which plays in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and VLC and uploads directly to YouTube.

Screen recording requires a desktop browser—mobile browsers don’t allow websites to capture the screen.

How to use the screen recorder

  1. Enter your details. Record your screen, a window or a browser tab online with system audio and microphone. No download, no watermark, no time limit. Save the video to your device.
  2. Get the result. Press Start recording . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.

Pertanyaan umum

Apakah ada tanda air atau batas waktu?

Juga tidak. Durasi perekaman hanya dibatasi oleh memori perangkat Anda.

Mengapa tidak ada suara?

Pastikan Anda mencentang “Bagikan audio tab” atau “Bagikan audio sistem” di dialog berbagi browser. macOS hanya mendukung audio tab.

Apakah rekaman saya sudah diunggah?

Tidak. Video dibuat dan disimpan di browser Anda sampai Anda mendownloadnya.

Apakah screen recorder gratis?

Ya. screen recorder gratis di browser, tanpa akun.

Apakah data saya keluar dari perangkat?

Sebagian besar alat memproses input di perangkat Anda. Jika alat memanggil API publik (misalnya umur domain), itu ditulis di halaman.
