How to use the cover letter AI

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Role & company and Your background (short) .
  2. Get the result. Press Draft cover letter . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.

When to use the cover letter AI

Draft a job cover letter from role and experience notes using Chrome’s Writer API. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
