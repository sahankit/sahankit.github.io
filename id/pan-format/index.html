Permanent Account Number layout

PANs are 10 characters: five letters, four digits, one letter. This tool checks format only. It does not query the Income Tax Department or prove a PAN is active.

How to use the PAN format check

  1. Enter your details. Fill in PAN .
  2. Get the result. Press Check format . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
