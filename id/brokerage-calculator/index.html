What is subtracted

Gross profit is the sell value minus the buy value. The calculator then subtracts brokerage twice, once for the buy order and once for the sell order, and STT on both the buy value and the sell value at the percent you enter.

A real contract note also has exchange transaction charges, GST on brokerage, stamp duty and a SEBI fee. Those differ by exchange and by state. Set brokerage to 0 if your plan does not charge it, and set STT to the rate in force on the day you trade.

How to use the brokerage calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Buy price (₹) , Sell price (₹) , Quantity , Brokerage per order (₹) and STT on buy and on sell (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.

Pertanyaan umum

Mengapa broker defaultnya adalah ₹20?

Beberapa broker diskon membatasi pesanan yang dibebankan sebesar ₹20. Paket pengiriman sering kali seharga ₹0. Ketikkan nomor dari halaman harga broker Anda.

Apakah 0,1% STT yang benar?

0,1% di kedua sisi adalah angka penyerahan ekuitas yang biasa digunakan orang. STT Intraday dikenakan biaya berbeda, seringkali hanya di sisi penjualan. Ubah bidang agar sesuai dengan segmen.

Apakah brokerage calculator gratis?

Ya. brokerage calculator gratis di browser, tanpa akun.

Apakah data saya keluar dari perangkat?

Sebagian besar alat memproses input di perangkat Anda. Jika alat memanggil API publik (misalnya umur domain), itu ditulis di halaman.
