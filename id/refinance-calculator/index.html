About the refinance calculator

Compare monthly payment and interest between your current mortgage and a refinanced loan for the remaining term.

Berjalan di browser—tanpa daftar atau instal.

How to use the refinance calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Remaining loan balance ($) , Current rate (%) , New rate (%) , Years left on loan and Refinance closing costs ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Compare loans . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
