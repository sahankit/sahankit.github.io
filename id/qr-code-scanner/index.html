Two ways to scan

Camera: press Scan with camera and point it at the QR code. On phones the rear camera is used. The code is read automatically and the camera stops.

Image: upload a screenshot or photo that contains a QR code—handy for codes received on WhatsApp or email, or when you are on a laptop without a camera.

Scan safely

Links are never opened automatically. Check the decoded address before tapping Buka tautan, and be careful with QR codes that ask for UPI payments or login details.

How to use the QR code scanner

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Scan with camera . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.

Pertanyaan umum

Bisakah saya memindai kode QR dari tangkapan layar?

Ya. Unggah tangkapan layar menggunakan pemilih file.

Apakah itu membaca kode QR UPI?

Ya, ini menampilkan teks tautan pembayaran UPI sehingga Anda dapat memverifikasi penerima pembayaran sebelum membayar di aplikasi UPI Anda.

Apakah ada yang diunggah?

Tidak. Decoding terjadi di browser Anda.

Apakah Pemindai kode QR gratis?

Ya. Pemindai kode QR gratis di browser, tanpa akun.

Apakah data saya keluar dari perangkat?

Sebagian besar alat memproses input di perangkat Anda. Jika alat memanggil API publik (misalnya umur domain), itu ditulis di halaman.
