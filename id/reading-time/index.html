Why show reading time on posts?

Readers choose articles partly by time commitment. A “5 min read” label sets expectations and can improve engagement when it matches your actual word count.

How to use the reading time

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Paste article text and Reading speed (words per minute) .
  2. Get the result. The result updates on the page as you type.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
