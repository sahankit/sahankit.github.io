About the EITC calculator

Rough US earned income tax credit estimate for tax year 2024 from income and number of qualifying children.

Berjalan di browser—tanpa daftar atau instal.

How to use the EITC calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Earned income ($) and Qualifying children .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate EITC . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
