On-device summarization

Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI (Gemini Nano) and enough free disk space. Processing happens on your device.

Prefer the full experience? Use the Ringkasan AI page (same Chrome Summarizer API).

How to use the AI summarizer

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Summary type and Length .
  2. Get the result. Press Summarize . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.

When to use the AI summarizer

Summarize long text with Chrome’s built-in Summarizer API. Key points stay on your device. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
