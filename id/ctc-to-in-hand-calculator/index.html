About the CTC to in-hand

In-hand salary from annual CTC using FY 2026–27 new-regime tax (87A rebate, ₹75,000 standard deduction) plus a simple PF placeholder.

Berjalan di browser—tanpa daftar atau instal.

How to use the CTC to in-hand

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Annual CTC (₹) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
