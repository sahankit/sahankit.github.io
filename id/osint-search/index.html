          What is this OSINT search page?

          OSINT (open-source intelligence) means gathering information from publicly available sources—websites, DNS, certificate transparency logs, search engines, and archives. This hub does not scrape data or bypass logins. It builds URLs you can open in a new tab to continue research on third-party services.

          Use it for legitimate purposes: securing your own accounts, bug-bounty scope checks, journalism, fraud investigation with authorization, and competitive research on public marketing sites.

          Sections in the OSINT hub

          Each block targets a common OSINT workflow:

          • Pencarian nama pengguna — Quick links to public profile URLs on GitHub, Reddit, X, and similar sites, plus a Google query for pasted leaks (public index only).
          • Domain OSINT — Certificate search (crt.sh), DNS, WHOIS-style pages, VirusTotal, and our domain age checker.
          • Surel — Link to Have I Been Pwned and public search helpers (not verification of mailbox access).
          • IP — Routing and reputation pages such as ipinfo.io and BGP tools.
          • Pembuat Google bodoh — Compose site: and filetype: style queries for authorized testing.
          • Mesin Wayback — Historical snapshots of a URL on archive.org.

          Ethics and legal use

          Do not use OSINT techniques to harass, stalk, or access private data without permission. Laws vary by country; when in doubt, get written authorization or stick to your own assets and public bug-bounty programs. This site is not responsible for how third-party sites use your queries.

          How to use the OSINT search

          1. Enter your details. Fill in Username or profile URL , Domain , Email address , IPv4 or IPv6 and site: (optional) .
          2. Get the result. Press Build links . Results update on the page.
          3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.

          Pertanyaan umum

          Apakah alat OSINT ini mencari secara otomatis?

          Tidak. Itu hanya menghasilkan tautan. Anda mengklik ke setiap situs eksternal. Tidak ada pertanyaan yang dikirim ke server kami.

          Apakah OSINT sah?

          Penelitian terhadap informasi yang dipublikasikan secara publik sering kali bersifat legal, namun konteksnya penting. Akses tidak sah ke sistem, melewati paywall, atau menyalahgunakan data pribadi dapat dianggap ilegal. Gunakan pedoman etika dan hukum setempat.

          Bisakah saya menemukan kata sandi email pribadi seseorang di sini?

          Tidak. Halaman ini tidak menyediakan peretasan kata sandi, pelanggaran unduhan, atau akses ke database non-publik.

          Apa itu Google bodoh?

          Operator pencarian lanjutan (site:, filetype:, intitle:, dll.) yang membantu menemukan halaman publik yang diindeks oleh Google. Gunakan hanya pada sistem yang mungkin Anda uji.

          Apakah Pencarian OSINT gratis?

          Ya. Pencarian OSINT gratis di browser, tanpa akun.

          Apakah data saya keluar dari perangkat?

          Sebagian besar alat memproses input di perangkat Anda. Jika alat memanggil API publik (misalnya umur domain), itu ditulis di halaman.
