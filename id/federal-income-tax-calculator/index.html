About the federal income tax calculator

Estimate US federal income tax for a single filer using 2024 brackets and standard deduction. Simplified — not a full 1040.

Berjalan di browser—tanpa daftar atau instal.

How to use the federal income tax calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Taxable wages ($ / year) , Standard deduction ($) and Pre-tax 401(k) ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate tax . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Salin atau unduh hasil. Tidak disimpan—segarkan halaman untuk mulai ulang.
