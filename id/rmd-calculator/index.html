About the RMD calculator

Estimate required minimum distribution from a traditional IRA or 401(k) using IRS uniform lifetime divisor by age.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the RMD calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Usiamu dan Saldo akun 31 Desember sebelumnya ($).
  2. Get the result. Press Hitung RMD. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
