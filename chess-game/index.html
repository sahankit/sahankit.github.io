How to play

Tap or click a piece to see legal moves, then tap a highlighted square to move. White moves first. Use Flip board if you prefer black at the bottom.

In vs computer mode you play White. The computer plays a simple heuristic — good for casual games, not a master.

How to use the chess game

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Mode.
  2. Get the result. Press New game. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Does this follow real chess rules?

Yes: legal moves only, castling, en passant, pawn promotion to queen, check, checkmate, and stalemate.

Can two people play on one phone?

Choose Two players (same screen) in the mode menu.

Is there a clock or rating?

No timer or Elo rating. It is a casual board for practice and fun.

Is this chess game free?

Yes. The chess game is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
