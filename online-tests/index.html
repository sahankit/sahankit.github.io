Frequently asked questions

Are webcam and mic tests private?

Yes. Video and audio are processed only in your browser and never uploaded.

Why does my browser ask for permission?

Websites need your permission to use the camera or microphone. Access ends when you close the tab or press Stop.

Do these tests work on phones?

Yes. Webcam, mic, CPS, reaction time and screen tests work on modern mobile browsers.

Which server does the speed test use?

It measures against Cloudflare’s public speed-test network, picking a nearby location automatically. Only test data is transferred.
