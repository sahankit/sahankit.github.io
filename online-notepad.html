A simple notepad that remembers

Start typing and your note is saved to this browser’s local storage within a second. Close the tab, come back later, and it is still there. Word and character counts update as you write.

Use Download .txt to keep a copy on your device, or Copy all to paste into email, chat or a document.

Privacy

Notes never leave your device—there is no account and no server copy. That also means notes do not sync between devices, and clearing your browser data deletes them, so download anything important.

How to use the online notepad

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Your note (saved automatically in this browser).
  2. Get the result. Press Download .txt. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my note stored?

In your browser’s localStorage on this device only.

Will it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, you can keep typing and saving without a connection.

Can I open it in two tabs?

Yes. Changes made in one tab are picked up by the other.

Is this online notepad free?

Yes. The online notepad is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
