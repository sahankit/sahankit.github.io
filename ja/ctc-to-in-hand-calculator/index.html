About the CTC to in-hand

How to use the CTC to in-hand

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 年間 CTC (₹) そして 合計控除額 (CTC の%).
  2. Get the result. Press 見積もり. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this CTC to in-hand free?

Yes. The CTC to in-hand is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
