How to use the running pace

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Distance (km) and Time (minutes) .
  2. Get the result. Press Pace . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。

When to use the running pace

Calculate pace from distance and time. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
