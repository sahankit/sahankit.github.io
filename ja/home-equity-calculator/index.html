About the home equity calculator

Estimate home equity, loan-to-value ratio, and borrowable equity at a max LTV for US homeowners.

ブラウザで動作—登録もインストールも不要。

How to use the home equity calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Home value ($) , Mortgage balance ($) and Max LTV for borrowing (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate equity . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
