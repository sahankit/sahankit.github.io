Chrome built-in grammar check

Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI (Gemini Nano) and enough free disk space. Processing happens on your device.

Paste text and run Check grammar to see suggested fixes from the Proofreader API.

How to use the grammar checker

  1. Enter your details. Check grammar, spelling, and punctuation with Chrome’s built-in Proofreader API. Private, on-device when supported.
  2. Get the result. Press Check grammar . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
