About the LTA calculator

Estimate how much of your Leave Travel Allowance can be exempt from tax when travel costs and LTA received are known. Uses common salary-tax assumptions.

ブラウザで動作—登録もインストールも不要。

How to use the LTA calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in LTA received from employer (₹ / year) , Actual eligible travel cost (₹) and Travel journeys claimed this block (max 2) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate exemption . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
