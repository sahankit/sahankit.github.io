Average human reaction time

Most people react to a visual cue in about 200–300 milliseconds, with a typical average near 250 ms. Under 200 ms is exceptional. Results also include the delay of your mouse, screen and browser, so a high-refresh monitor and wired mouse give slightly faster readings.

How the test works

Click the box to start. It turns red and waits a random 1–4.5 seconds, then turns green. Click as soon as you see green. Clicking early counts as “too soon” and is not scored. After five valid tries you get your average.

How to use the reaction time test

  1. Enter your details. Free reaction time test: click when the box turns green and see your reaction speed in milliseconds. Five-try average, best score, and how you compare.
  2. Get the result. The result updates on the page as you type.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

キーボードを使ってもいいですか？

はい。ボックスにフォーカスし、クリックする代わりに Space キーまたは Enter キーを押します。

携帯電話では結果が遅くなるのはなぜですか?

タッチスクリーンと携帯電話のブラウザでは、入力遅延が多少追加されます。同じデバイス上のスコアを比較します。

睡眠やカフェインは反応時間に影響しますか?

はい。疲労により反応が著しく遅くなります。カフェインはそれらをわずかに改善することができます。さまざまな時間にテストして確認してください。

Is this reaction time test free?

Yes. The reaction time test is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
