How SIP maturity is estimated

The calculator assumes a fixed monthly investment and a constant annual return, compounded monthly. Real mutual fund returns vary; use this for illustration and planning only.

How to use the SIP calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 月額 SIP (₹), 年 そして 期待収益率 (%/年).
  2. Get the result. Press 計算する. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
