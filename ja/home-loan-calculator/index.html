Home loan EMI formula

Monthly EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula on principal, annual interest rate, and tenure in years. Results are estimates—banks may add fees, insurance, or floating rates.

How to use the home loan EMI

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 融資金額 (₹), 金利 (%/年) そして 在職期間（年）.
  2. Get the result. Press EMIの計算. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
