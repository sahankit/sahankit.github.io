  1. Enter your details. Fill in All corners (px) , Top left (px) , Top right (px) , Bottom right (px) and Bottom left (px) .
  2. Get the result. The result updates on the page as you type.

When to use the border radius generator

Set each corner in pixels, preview the box, and copy the border-radius CSS. One value rounds all four corners. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
