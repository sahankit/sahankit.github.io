About the TDEE calculator

Calculate total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) and BMR using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Free calorie calculator.

ブラウザで動作—登録もインストールも不要。

How to use the TDEE calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Sex , Age (years) , Weight (kg) , Height (cm) and Activity .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
