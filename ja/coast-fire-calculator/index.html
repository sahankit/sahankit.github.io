About the coast FIRE calculator

See whether invested assets are enough to grow to your FIRE number without more contributions, or how many years of saving remain at your current rate.

ブラウザで動作—登録もインストールも不要。

How to use the coast FIRE calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Annual spending in retirement ($) , Safe withdrawal rate (%) , Invested assets today ($) , Monthly savings ($) and Expected annual return (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Check coast FIRE . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
