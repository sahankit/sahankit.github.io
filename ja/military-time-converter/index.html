About the military time

Convert between military time (24-hour) and standard 12-hour AM/PM time. Free time format converter.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the military time

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 24 時間制 (HH:MM) そして 12時間の結果.
  2. Get the result. Press 変換する. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this military time free?

Yes. The military time is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
