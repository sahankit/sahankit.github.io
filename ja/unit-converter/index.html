How to use the unit converter

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Type , Value and Direction .
  2. Get the result. Press Convert . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。

When to use the unit converter

Convert km/miles, kg/lb, and Celsius/Fahrenheit online. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
