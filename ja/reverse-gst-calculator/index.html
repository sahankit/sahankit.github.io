About the reverse GST calculator

Split a GST-inclusive price into taxable value and GST amount for rates 5%, 12%, 18% or 28%.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the reverse GST calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in GST 込みの金額 (₹) そして GST率(%).
  2. Get the result. Press 分割金額. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this reverse GST calculator free?

Yes. The reverse GST calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
