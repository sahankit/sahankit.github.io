About the wallpaper calculator

How to use the wallpaper calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Wall area (sq ft) , Roll coverage (sq ft) and Waste (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate rolls . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
