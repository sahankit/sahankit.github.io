About the child tax credit calculator

Estimate US child tax credit from number of qualifying children and modified adjusted gross income.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the child tax credit calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Qualifying children under 17, MAGI ($) そして Filing.
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate CTC. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Yes. The child tax credit calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.
