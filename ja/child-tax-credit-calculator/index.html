About the child tax credit calculator

Estimate US child tax credit from number of qualifying children and modified adjusted gross income.

ブラウザで動作—登録もインストールも不要。

How to use the child tax credit calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Qualifying children under 17 , MAGI ($) and Filing .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate CTC . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
