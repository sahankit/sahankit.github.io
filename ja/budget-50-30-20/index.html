50/30/20 予算計算ツール

Split monthly take-home pay into 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings. Change the three shares if you want a different split.

必要なものは家賃、食費、通勤交通費、請求書です。欲しいものは外食と買い物です。貯蓄には投資と緊急資金が含まれます。 3 つの株式を合計すると 100 になります。

What goes in each bucket

Needs are the bills you pay to keep the household going: rent, groceries, utilities, insurance and the commute. Wants are the spending you could cut: eating out, subscriptions, shopping. Savings is the money that leaves the spending account: an emergency fund, EPF top-up, mutual funds.

In a high-rent city, needs can be more than half of take-home pay. Change the three percentages. They have to add up to 100 so the rupee amounts still equal your pay.

How to use the 50/30/20 budget

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 月々の手取り額（₹）, ニーズ (%), 欲しいもの (%) そして 節約 (%).
  2. Get the result. Press 給料を分割する. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Yes. The 50/30/20 budget is free to use in your browser with no account required.
