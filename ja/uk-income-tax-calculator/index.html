About the UK income tax

Estimate UK income tax on annual earnings for England, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

How to use the UK income tax

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Annual income (£) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate tax . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
