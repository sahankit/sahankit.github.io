    Detect language

    Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI (Gemini Nano) and enough free disk space. Processing happens on your device.

    How to use the language detector

    1. Enter your details. Detect the language of text with Chrome’s Language Detector API.
    2. Get the result. Press Detect . Results update on the page.
    3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。

    When to use the language detector

    Detect the language of text with Chrome’s Language Detector API. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

    Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
