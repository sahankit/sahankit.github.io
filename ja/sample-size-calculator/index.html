About the sample size

Estimate survey sample size for a proportion with margin of error. Free statistics sample calculator.

ブラウザで動作—登録もインストールも不要。

How to use the sample size

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Expected proportion (0–1, use 0.5 if unsure) , Margin of error (e.g. 0.05 = ±5%) and Z-score (1.96 = 95%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Sample size n . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
