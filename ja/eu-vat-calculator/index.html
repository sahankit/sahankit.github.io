About the EU VAT by country

Calculate VAT using standard VAT rates for EU countries: Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, and more.

How to use the EU VAT by country

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Amount ex VAT (€) and Country .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
