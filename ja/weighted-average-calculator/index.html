About the weighted average calculator

Calculate a weighted average from scores and percentage weights (weights should total 100).

ブラウザで動作—登録もインストールも不要。

How to use the weighted average calculator

  1. Enter your details. Calculate a weighted average from scores and percentage weights (weights should total 100).
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 結果をコピーまたはダウンロード。保存されません。再読み込みでやり直し。
