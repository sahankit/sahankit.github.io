About the hours calculator

Add hours and minutes from multiple time entries. Free timesheet hours calculator for work and billing.

How to use the hours calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Time 1 (hours:minutes) , Time 2 and Time 3 (optional) .
  2. Get the result. Press Add times . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.
