How to use the CPM calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Spend and Impressions .
  2. Get the result. Press CPM . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.

When to use the CPM calculator

Calculate CPM from ad spend and impressions. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
