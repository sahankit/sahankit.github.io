Häufige Fragen

Are these official EU or HMRC calculators?

No. Quick estimates for planning. UK stamp duty and tax rules change; confirm with HMRC or your tax authority.

Do tools include reduced VAT rates?

Most pages use standard VAT. Food, books, and cross-border B2B supplies have special rules.
