About the hourly wage calculator

Convert an annual salary to an hourly wage or an hourly rate to yearly pay. Uses 40 hours per week and 52 weeks unless you change them.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the hourly wage calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Konvertieren, Betrag ($), Stunden pro Woche und Wochen pro Jahr.
  2. Get the result. Press Konvertieren. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this hourly wage calculator free?

Yes. The hourly wage calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
