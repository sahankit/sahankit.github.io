About the salary slip

Generate a basic printable salary slip with earnings and deductions. Browser template—not payroll software.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the salary slip

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Name des Mitarbeiters, Grundpreis (£), HRA (£), Abzüge (£) und Ab Monat.
  2. Get the result. Press Vorschau-Slip(s). Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this salary slip free?

Yes. The salary slip is free to use in your browser with no account required.

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
