How to use the vCard generator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Full name , Organization , Phone and Email .
  2. Get the result. Press Generate . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.

When to use the vCard generator

Generate a vCard 3.0 string for contacts. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
