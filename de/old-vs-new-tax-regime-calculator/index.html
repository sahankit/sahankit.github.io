About the old vs new tax regime

Compare estimated income tax under India's old and new personal tax regimes for FY 2025-26. Includes standard deduction and common 80C/HRA inputs for the old regime.

Läuft im Browser—keine Anmeldung, keine Installation.

How to use the old vs new tax regime

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Gross salary (₹ / year) , 80C + 80CCD(1B) etc. (₹, old regime) and HRA + other exemptions (₹, old regime) .
  2. Get the result. Press Compare tax . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.
