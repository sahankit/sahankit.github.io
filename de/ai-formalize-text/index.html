Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI and enough free disk space. Text is processed on your device—not on Sahankit servers.

How to use the formalize text

  1. Enter your details. Make informal text more formal with Chrome’s Rewriter API. Good for emails and reports.
  2. Get the result. Press Formalize . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.
