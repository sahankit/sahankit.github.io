Estimate your due date from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). Free pregnancy calculator—illustration only, not medical advice.

How to use the pregnancy due date

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Erster Tag der letzten Periode (LMP).
  2. Get the result. Press Fälligkeitsdatum berechnen. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
