How to use the CIDR calculator
  1. Enter your details. Fill in IPv4 CIDR .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.
When to use the CIDR calculator
IPv4 CIDR notation: network address, mask, and host count. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.
