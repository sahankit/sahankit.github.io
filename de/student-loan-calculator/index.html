About the student loan calculator

Estimate the monthly payment and total interest on a student loan balance at a fixed annual rate.

Läuft im Browser—keine Anmeldung, keine Installation.

How to use the student loan calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Loan balance ($) , Annual interest rate (%) and Repayment (years) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate payment . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.
