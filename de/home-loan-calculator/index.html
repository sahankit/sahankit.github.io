Home loan EMI formula

Monthly EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula on principal, annual interest rate, and tenure in years. Results are estimates—banks may add fees, insurance, or floating rates.

How to use the home loan EMI

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Loan amount (₹) , Interest rate (% per year) and Tenure (years) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate EMI . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Ergebnis kopieren oder herunterladen. Nichts wird gespeichert—neu laden zum Zurücksetzen.

When to use the home loan EMI

Free home loan EMI calculator for India. Monthly EMI, total interest, and total payment from loan amount, rate, and tenure. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
