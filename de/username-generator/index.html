How the ideas are made
The name is lowercased and split into words. The suggestions join those words, separate them with a dot or an underscore, and add a short number or the current year. Letters and digits are kept. Other characters are dropped.
Instagram, Gmail, games and banks each have their own length rules and taken names. This page does not look any of them up.

When to use the username generator
Turn a name into username ideas with dots, underscores and numbers. Nothing is checked against Instagram, Gmail or any other site. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.
Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
