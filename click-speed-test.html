What is a good CPS score?

Shorter tests (1–5 seconds) measure burst speed; 30 and 60 second tests measure endurance. Your best score for each length is saved in this browser.

  • Under 4.5 CPS — casual clicking
  • 4.5 to 6.5 CPS — average for most people
  • 6.5 to 8 CPS — above average
  • 8 to 10 CPS — excellent; common with jitter clicking
  • 10+ CPS — very fast; usually butterfly or drag clicking

Tips to click faster

Rest your wrist, tense your forearm slightly for jitter clicking, or use two fingers alternately (butterfly clicking). A lightweight mouse with a crisp switch helps. On phones and tablets, tap with two thumbs.

Frequently asked questions

How is CPS calculated?

Total clicks divided by the test length in seconds. The timer starts on your first click.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Taps are counted the same way as mouse clicks.

Why did my score reset?

Changing the test length starts a fresh run. Best scores are stored separately for each length.
