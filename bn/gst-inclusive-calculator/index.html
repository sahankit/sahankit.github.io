Inclusive vs exclusive quick examples

  • ₹1,050 inclusive @ 5% → taxable ≈ ₹1,000
  • ₹1,120 inclusive @ 12% → taxable ≈ ₹1,000
  • ₹1,180 inclusive @ 18% → taxable ≈ ₹1,000
  • ₹1,280 inclusive @ 28% → taxable ≈ ₹1,000

How to use the GST inclusive price

  1. Enter your details. Fill in GST-inclusive amount (₹) and GST rate (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Split amount . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. আউটপুট কপি বা ডাউনলোড করুন। কিছু সেভ হয় না—রিফ্রেশ করে আবার শুরু করুন।

When to use the GST inclusive price

Find taxable value and GST amount from a GST-inclusive price. India GST 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% rates. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.
