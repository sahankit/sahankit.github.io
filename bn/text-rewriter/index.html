Rewrite with Chrome AI

Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI (Gemini Nano) and enough free disk space. Processing happens on your device.

How to use the AI rewriter

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Tone and Length .
  2. Get the result. Press Rewrite . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. আউটপুট কপি বা ডাউনলোড করুন। কিছু সেভ হয় না—রিফ্রেশ করে আবার শুরু করুন।

When to use the AI rewriter

Rewrite text to be shorter, longer, or more formal with Chrome’s Rewriter API. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
