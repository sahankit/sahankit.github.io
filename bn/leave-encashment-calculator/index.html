About the leave encashment

Rough leave encashment from basic salary and unused leave days. Illustration for salary planning—not payroll advice.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the leave encashment

  1. Enter your details. Fill in মাসিক মৌলিক (₹) এবং অব্যবহৃত ছুটির দিন.
  2. Get the result. Press অনুমান. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this leave encashment free?

Yes. The leave encashment is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
