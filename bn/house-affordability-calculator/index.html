About the house affordability

Estimate affordable home price using income and DTI-style payment ratio. Illustration—not a loan offer.

How to use the house affordability

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Gross monthly income ($) , Max housing payment (% of income) , Down payment ($) , Mortgage rate (% APR) and Loan term (years) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. আউটপুট কপি বা ডাউনলোড করুন। কিছু সেভ হয় না—রিফ্রেশ করে আবার শুরু করুন।
