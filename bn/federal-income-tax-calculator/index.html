How to use the federal income tax calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in করযোগ্য মজুরি ($/বছর), স্ট্যান্ডার্ড ডিডাকশন ($) এবং প্রি-ট্যাক্স 401(k) ($).
  2. Get the result. Press অনুমান কর. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this federal income tax calculator free?

Yes. The federal income tax calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
