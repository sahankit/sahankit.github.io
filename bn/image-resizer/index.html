How to use the image resizer

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Resize & download . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. আউটপুট কপি বা ডাউনলোড করুন। কিছু সেভ হয় না—রিফ্রেশ করে আবার শুরু করুন।

When to use the image resizer

Resize images by max width while keeping aspect ratio. Download PNG. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
