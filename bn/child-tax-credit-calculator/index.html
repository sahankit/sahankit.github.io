How to use the child tax credit calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 17 বছরের কম বয়সী যোগ্য শিশু, MAGI ($) এবং ফাইলিং.
  2. Get the result. Press সিটিসি অনুমান. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.

Frequently asked questions

Is this child tax credit calculator free?

Yes. The child tax credit calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
