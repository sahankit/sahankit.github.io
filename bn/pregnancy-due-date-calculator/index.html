About the pregnancy due date

Estimate your due date from the first day of your last menstrual period (LMP). Free pregnancy calculator—illustration only, not medical advice.

How to use the pregnancy due date

  1. Enter your details. Fill in First day of last period (LMP) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate due date . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. আউটপুট কপি বা ডাউনলোড করুন। কিছু সেভ হয় না—রিফ্রেশ করে আবার শুরু করুন।
