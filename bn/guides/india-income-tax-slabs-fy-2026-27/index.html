আয়কর স্ল্যাব FY 2026-27

সঙ্গে শাসন তুলনা আয়কর ক্যালকুলেটর and এইচআরএ ক্যালকুলেটর. এছাড়াও দেখুন অগ্রিম কর প্রদানের তারিখ.

For FY 2026–27, the new regime uses stepped slabs from nil up to ₹4 lakh through 30% above ₹24 lakh taxable income, with a ₹75,000 salary standard deduction and section 87A up to ₹12 lakh taxable income. The old regime keeps the classic 5%/20%/30% slabs and allows Chapter VI-A deductions such as 80C and HRA.

New tax regime slabs (resident < 60)

Standard deduction on salary is ₹75,000 in the new regime. Section 87A can zero out tax when taxable income (after standard deduction) does not exceed ₹12 lakh, subject to current law. Slab summary: 0–₹4L nil; ₹4–8L 5%; ₹8–12L 10%; ₹12–16L 15%; ₹16–20L 20%; ₹20–24L 25%; above ₹24L 30%.

  • ₹0–4 lakh: nil
  • ₹4–8 lakh: 5%
  • ₹8–12 lakh: 10%
  • ₹12–16 lakh: 15%
  • ₹16–20 lakh: 20%
  • ₹20–24 lakh: 25%
  • Above ₹24 lakh: 30%

Old tax regime slabs (resident < 60)

Standard deduction on salary is ₹50,000. Eligible deductions under Chapter VI-A (80C, 80D, HRA, housing loan interest, etc.) apply only in the old regime.

  • ₹0–2.5 lakh: nil
  • ₹2.5–5 lakh: 5%
  • ₹5–10 lakh: 20%
  • Above ₹10 lakh: 30%

Cess and disclaimer

Health and education cess at 4% applies on tax after rebate. Surcharge and special incomes are not covered here. Last updated for illustration tools on ToolHQ—verify before filing.

সাধারণ প্রশ্ন

Which regime is default for salaried employees?

The new regime is often the default unless you opt into the old regime through your employer or return, subject to current rules.

Can I switch regime every year?

Many salaried taxpayers can choose each financial year. Business taxpayers may face lock-in—check current Income Tax rules.
