About the CM to feet

Convert centimeters to feet and inches (and back). Free height converter for body measurements and travel.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the CM to feet

  1. Enter your details. Fill in সেন্টিমিটার এবং ফুট এবং ইঞ্চি.
  2. Get the result. Press রূপান্তর করুন. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this CM to feet free?

Yes. The CM to feet is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
