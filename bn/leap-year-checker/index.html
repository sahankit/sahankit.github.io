The rule

A year is a leap year when it divides by 4. If it also divides by 100, it is not a leap year, unless it divides by 400. 2024 and 2028 are leap years. 2100 is not.

  2. Get the result. Press Check year . Results update on the page.

When to use the leap year checker

Check whether a year is a leap year. A year is a leap year when it is divisible by 4, except century years, which must be divisible by 400. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.
