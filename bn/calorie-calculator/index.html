About the calorie calculator

Estimate daily calorie needs for weight maintenance from age, sex, weight, height, and activity.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the calorie calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in সেক্স, বয়স (বছর), ওজন (কেজি), উচ্চতা (সেমি) এবং কার্যকলাপ.
  2. Get the result. Press দৈনিক ক্যালোরি. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this calorie calculator free?

Yes. The calorie calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
