How to test your microphone

Press Test my microphone and allow access. Speak normally—the level bar and waveform should move. A level around −30 to −10 dBFS while talking is healthy; constantly near 0 means the input is too loud and may distort.

Press Record 5 seconds to capture a short clip and play it back to hear how you sound on calls. Echo cancellation and noise suppression are turned off so you hear the raw microphone.

Microphone not detected?

  • Choose the correct device from the Microphone list (headset vs. built-in).
  • Check mute switches on headsets and the system input volume.
  • Allow microphone permission for this site in the address bar.
  • Close other apps that may be holding the microphone, then retry.

How to use the mic test

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Microphone.
  2. Get the result. Press Test my microphone. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is my voice uploaded?

No. Audio is analysed and recorded only in your browser and is discarded when you leave the page.

What does dBFS mean?

Decibels relative to full scale. 0 dBFS is the loudest possible signal; quieter sounds are negative numbers.

Can I test AirPods or Bluetooth headsets?

Yes. Connect them first, then select them in the Microphone list after granting permission.

Is this mic test free?

Yes. The mic test is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
