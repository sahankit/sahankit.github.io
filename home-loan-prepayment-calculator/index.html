Reduce tenure or reduce EMI?

When you prepay, most banks let you either keep the EMI and finish the loan sooner (reduce tenure) or keep the end date and pay a smaller EMI (reduce EMI). Reducing tenure almost always saves far more interest, because the lower balance stops accruing interest for the remaining years.

Example: on a ₹30 lakh loan at 8.5% for 20 years, (EMI about ₹26,035), a single ₹2 lakh prepayment after 12 months saves about ₹6.9 lakh in interest and ends the loan 34 months early if you keep the EMI.

When prepayment makes sense

  • Prepay early—the first years of a loan are mostly interest.
  • Floating-rate home loans taken by individuals have no prepayment penalty under RBI rules.
  • Keep an emergency fund before prepaying, and compare with investing if your expected returns beat the loan rate after tax.
  • Remember the tax deduction on home loan interest (Section 24b) falls as you prepay.

How to use the home loan prepayment

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Outstanding loan amount (₹), Interest rate (% per year), Remaining tenure (years), Prepayment amount (₹) and Prepayment frequency.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a penalty for prepaying a home loan?

Not for floating-rate home loans to individuals. Fixed-rate loans may charge a fee—check your loan agreement.

How is interest saved calculated?

The tool simulates every monthly instalment with and without prepayment and compares total interest paid.

Can I model a yearly bonus prepayment?

Yes. Set frequency to Every year and choose the month of the first prepayment.

Is this home loan prepayment free?

Yes. The home loan prepayment is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
