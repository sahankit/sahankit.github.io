How to use the AI translator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Text , From , To and Translation .
  2. Get the result. Press Translate . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.

When to use the AI translator

Translate text with Chrome’s Translator API. Private on-device translation—no upload to Sahankit. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
