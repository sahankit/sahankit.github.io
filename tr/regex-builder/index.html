How to use the regex builder
  1. Enter your details. Fill in Preset templates , Insert escaped literal , Regular expression and Sample text .
  2. Get the result. Press Test matches . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.
Sık sorulan sorular
How is this different from the regex tester?
The builder focuses on assembling patterns from presets and parts; the tester is minimal paste-and-run. Both run locally.
Which flavor of regex?
JavaScript RegExp (same as in browsers and Node). Flags g, i, m, and s are supported here.
