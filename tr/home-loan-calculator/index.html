Home loan EMI formula

Monthly EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula on principal, annual interest rate, and tenure in years. Results are estimates—banks may add fees, insurance, or floating rates.

How to use the home loan EMI

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Loan amount (₹) , Interest rate (% per year) and Tenure (years) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate EMI . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.
