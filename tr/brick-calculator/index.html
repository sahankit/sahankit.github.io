How to use the brick calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Wall area (sq ft) , Brick face area (sq in) and Mortar joint (in) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate bricks . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.
