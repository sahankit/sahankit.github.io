Unix time in seconds and milliseconds

Timestamps from logs and APIs are often Unix epoch values. Values below 1,000,000,000,000 are treated as seconds and multiplied by 1,000. Larger values are treated as milliseconds.

How to use the epoch converter

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Timestamp (seconds or ms) .
  2. Get the result. Press Convert . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.
