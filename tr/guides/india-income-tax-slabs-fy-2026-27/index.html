Income tax slabs FY 2026–27

Compare regimes with the income tax calculator and HRA calculator. See also advance tax due dates.

For FY 2026–27, the new regime uses stepped slabs from nil up to ₹3 lakh through 30% above ₹15 lakh taxable income, with a higher salary standard deduction. The old regime keeps the classic 5%/20%/30% slabs and allows Chapter VI-A deductions such as 80C and HRA.

New tax regime slabs (resident < 60)

Standard deduction on salary is ₹75,000 in the new regime. Section 87A can zero out tax when total income (after standard deduction) does not exceed ₹7 lakh, subject to current law.

  • ₹0–3 lakh: nil
  • ₹3–7 lakh: 5%
  • ₹7–10 lakh: 10%
  • ₹10–12 lakh: 15%
  • ₹12–15 lakh: 20%
  • Above ₹15 lakh: 30%

Old tax regime slabs (resident < 60)

Standard deduction on salary is ₹50,000. Eligible deductions under Chapter VI-A (80C, 80D, HRA, housing loan interest, etc.) apply only in the old regime.

  • ₹0–2.5 lakh: nil
  • ₹2.5–5 lakh: 5%
  • ₹5–10 lakh: 20%
  • Above ₹10 lakh: 30%

Cess and disclaimer

Health and education cess at 4% applies on tax after rebate. Surcharge and special incomes are not covered here. Last updated for illustration tools on ToolHQ—verify before filing.

Sık sorulan sorular

Which regime is default for salaried employees?

The new regime is often the default unless you opt into the old regime through your employer or return, subject to current rules.

Can I switch regime every year?

Many salaried taxpayers can choose each financial year. Business taxpayers may face lock-in—check current Income Tax rules.
