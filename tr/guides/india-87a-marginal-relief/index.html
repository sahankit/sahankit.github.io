How 87A rebate and marginal relief work above ₹12 lakh

Under the new regime for FY 2026–27, section 87A (rebate under the Income-tax Act, 2025 — often cited as section 156 in the new Act) can reduce tax to nil when taxable income after the ₹75,000 salary standard deduction is up to ₹12 lakh. That is why a ₹12.75 lakh gross salary often shows zero tax.

Just above ₹12 lakh taxable, marginal relief caps tax so you never pay more extra tax than the rupees by which you crossed ₹12 lakh (before 4% cess). Try ₹12.80 LPA in the income tax calculator.
