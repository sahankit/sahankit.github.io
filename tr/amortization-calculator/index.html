How to use the amortization calculator
  1. Enter your details. Fill in Loan amount ($) , Annual interest rate (%) and Term (years) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.
Sık sorulan sorular
Does this include taxes or insurance?
No. It is principal and interest only, like a bare mortgage payment.
Why show only 12 months?
A full 30-year table is huge. The summary still uses the full term for total interest.
