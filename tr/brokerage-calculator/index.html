Brokerage calculator

Estimate delivery-trade profit after a flat brokerage per order and STT. Defaults are ₹20 an order and 0.1% STT. Change both if your broker or the tax rule differs.

Two orders are charged: one buy and one sell. Exchange fees, GST on brokerage, stamp duty and SEBI charges are not included. STT of 0.1% is a common delivery rate — confirm the rate in force on the trade date.

What is subtracted

Gross profit is the sell value minus the buy value. The calculator then subtracts brokerage twice, once for the buy order and once for the sell order, and STT on both the buy value and the sell value at the percent you enter.

A real contract note also has exchange transaction charges, GST on brokerage, stamp duty and a SEBI fee. Those differ by exchange and by state. Set brokerage to 0 if your plan does not charge it, and set STT to the rate in force on the day you trade.

How to use the brokerage calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Buy price (₹) , Sell price (₹) , Quantity , Brokerage per order (₹) and STT on buy and on sell (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.

Sık sorulan sorular

Why is the default brokerage ₹20?

Several discount brokers cap a charged order at ₹20. Delivery plans are often ₹0. Type the number from your broker’s pricing page.

Is 0.1% the right STT?

0.1% on both sides is the usual equity-delivery figure people still use. Intraday STT is charged differently, often only on the sell side. Change the field to match the segment.
