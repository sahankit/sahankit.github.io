Compress image under 50 KB or 100 KB for forms

Government and exam portals often cap photo uploads at 20–50 KB while keeping minimum dimensions. This compressor reduces file size by re-encoding to JPEG at your chosen quality—start around 0.6–0.75 for portraits.

Need a precise target? Open Reduce image size in KB to hit 20 KB, 50 KB, or 100 KB with optional width × height limits.

Private compression (no cloud upload)

The image is drawn to a canvas and exported as JPEG in your browser. Nothing is sent to ToolHQ servers—suitable for ID proofs, signatures, and coursework scans.

How to use the image compressor

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Compress & download . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.

Sık sorulan sorular

How do I compress a photo under 50 KB?

Lower the quality slider and download. If still too large, use Reduce image size in KB with target 50 and crop excess background first.

PNG support?

You can upload PNG; output is JPEG for smaller size. Transparency becomes white in the JPEG.

Will compression change width and height?

This tool keeps original pixels and only changes JPEG quality. To resize dimensions, use the image resizer or reduce image size in KB tool.
