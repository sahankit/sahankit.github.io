How to use the carbon footprint

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Distance per month (km) and kg CO₂ per km .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Çıktıyı kopyalayın veya indirin. Saklanmaz—yenilemek sıfırlar.
