IFSC and PAN formats (India)

IFSC checker — 11 characters. PAN checker — 10 characters. GST calculator for invoice math. These tools do not verify with government databases.

Why format checkers are not verification

Format validation catches typos before you paste a code into a payment app. It cannot tell you whether a branch merged, a PAN is cancelled, or a name matches the account holder.
