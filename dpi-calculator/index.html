DPI calculator

Convert pixel width and print size into DPI, or turn inches and DPI into a pixel size. Useful for photos, posters and passport-size prints.

300 DPI is a common print target. Screens are often about 72–150 DPI. Passport photos are usually asked for in pixels or in millimetres, not DPI alone.

DPI from a photo

DPI is pixels divided by inches. A 1200-pixel-wide image printed 4 inches wide is 300 DPI. The same image printed 10 inches wide is 120 DPI and will look soft up close. The calculator also shows the width in millimetres.

Frequently asked questions

Is DPI the same as PPI?

For this sum, yes. PPI is pixels per inch on a screen or file. DPI is the matching print term. The number is pixels ÷ inches either way.

Is this DPI calculator free?

Yes. The DPI calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.
