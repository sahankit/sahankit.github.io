Frequently asked questions

Does this support .doc?

Only .docx (Office Open XML). Save older .doc files as .docx before converting.

Are images kept?

Embedded images in DOCX are usually preserved; very complex layouts may shift slightly in the PDF.

Is this word to PDF free?

Yes. The word to PDF is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
