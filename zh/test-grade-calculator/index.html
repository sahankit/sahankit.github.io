How to use the test grade calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 当前课程平均分 (%), 期末考试权重（课程百分比） 和 目标课程成绩（%）.
  2. Get the result. Press 计算. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
