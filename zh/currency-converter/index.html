How to use the currency converter

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 金额 和 率（1 从 = 率至）.
  2. Get the result. Press 转换. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
