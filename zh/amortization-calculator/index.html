About the amortization calculator
Monthly payment, total interest, and a short amortization schedule for a fixed-rate loan.
Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.
How to use the amortization calculator
  1. Enter your details. Fill in 贷款金额（元）, 年利率(%) 和 期限（年）.
  2. Get the result. Press 计算. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
Frequently asked questions
这包括税费或保险吗？
不。它只是本金和利息，就像裸抵押贷款一样。
为什么只显示12个月？
整整 30 年的表格是巨大的。摘要仍然使用完整的术语来表示总利息。
Is this amortization calculator free?
Yes. The amortization calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.
Does my data leave my device?
For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
