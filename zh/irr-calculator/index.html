About the IRR calculator

Estimate IRR from initial investment and yearly cash flows. Free internal rate of return calculator.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the IRR calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 初始投资（美元） 和 按年份划分的现金流入（以逗号分隔）.
  2. Get the result. Press 计算内部收益率. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
