Advance tax calendar

Estimate liability with the income tax calculator. Slab reference: FY 2025–26 slabs.

Advance tax is paid in four instalments—typically 15% by 15 June, 45% by 15 September, 75% by 15 December, and 100% by 15 March for the financial year.

Instalment schedule (FY)

If tax payable after TDS/TCS is ₹10,000 or more in a year, advance tax generally applies. Senior citizens without business income may be exempt—check current rules.

  • 15 June: 15% of annual tax liability
  • 15 September: 45% cumulative
  • 15 December: 75% cumulative
  • 15 March: 100% cumulative

Penalties (overview)

Section 234B may apply for short payment of advance tax; Section 234C for deferment of instalments. Pay through the Income Tax portal challan (advance tax) and keep receipts.

Does salary TDS count toward advance tax?

Employer TDS reduces your net advance tax due. Estimate balance liability with the income tax calculator.

Companies and many non-individual taxpayers follow the same quarterly pattern; verify for special cases.
