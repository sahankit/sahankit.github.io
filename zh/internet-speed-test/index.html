What your results mean

  • 下载（Mbps） — how fast data reaches you: streaming, browsing, downloads. Most plans are sold by this number.
  • 上传（Mbps） — how fast you send data: video calls, uploading files, live streaming.
  • 平（毫秒） — the delay for a round trip to the server. Under 30 ms feels instant; under 60 ms is fine for gaming.
  • 抖动（毫秒） — how much ping varies. Low jitter (under 10 ms) means smooth calls and games.

How much speed do you need?

The test downloads and uploads data over several connections for about eight seconds each, ignoring the first moments while the connection speeds up. For the most accurate result, use a cable or sit near the router, and pause other downloads and streaming.

  • Browsing and email: 3–5 Mbps
  • HD video (1080p) and video calls: 5–10 Mbps per device
  • 4K streaming: 25 Mbps per stream
  • Working from home with a family: 50–100 Mbps or more
  • Online gaming: speed matters less than low ping and jitter

常见问题

为什么我的速度低于我的计划？

Wi-Fi 距离和墙壁、使用该连接的其他设备、旧路由器和 VPN 都会降低速度。使用路由器旁边的电缆进行测试，以了解线路的实际速度。

Mbps 和 MB/s 有什么区别？

互联网速度以兆比特每秒 (Mbps) 为单位。除以 8 即可得到每秒兆字节数：100 Mbps ≈ 12.5 MB/s。

测试使用多少数据？

这取决于您的速度：典型连接上约为 20-50 MB，千兆光纤上可达数百 MB。
