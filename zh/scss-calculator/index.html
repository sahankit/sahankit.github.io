SCSS计算器

Estimate the quarterly interest from a Senior Citizen Savings Scheme deposit. Interest is paid out each quarter and is not compounded. Default rate 8.2% for October–December 2026.

SCSS 每季度支付利息。它不会添加回存款中，因此季度金额保持不变。 8.2%是2026年10月1日至12月31日期间通知的利率。该计划适用于60岁及以上的人，在某些退休情况下年龄较低。最高存款额由计划规则规定。

Why the quarterly amount does not grow

SCSS pays the interest out. It is not left in the account to compound. A ₹1,00,000 deposit at 8.2% pays ₹2,050 each quarter. Over 5 years that is ₹41,000 of interest, and the ₹1,00,000 comes back at maturity.

8.2% is the rate notified for 1 October to 31 December 2026. The rate on a new deposit is the rate of the quarter you open it. The scheme is for people aged 60 and above, with exceptions for some earlier retirees. There is a maximum deposit.

How to use the SCSS calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 押金（卢比）, 年率(%) 和 年.
  2. Get the result. Press 计算利息. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

利息要交税吗？

SCSS 利息需纳税。银行和邮局可能会在阈值以上扣除 TDS。此页面显示税前利息。

我可以延长吗？

基本期限为5年，规则允许延期。在年份字段中使用 8 仅作为较长付款的草图，并检查当前的延期规则。

Is this SCSS calculator free?

Yes. The SCSS calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
