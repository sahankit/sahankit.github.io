Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI and enough free disk space. Text is processed on your device—not on Sahankit servers.

How to use the AI email writer

  1. Enter your details. Fill in ईमेल में क्या लिखा होना चाहिए?, प्रसंग (वैकल्पिक) और सुर.
  2. Get the result. Press ईमेल लिखें. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this AI email writer free?

Yes. The AI email writer is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
