Estimate cash to seller after real estate commission, mortgage payoff, and closing costs.

How to use the seller net proceeds

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Sale price ($) , Commission (%) , Mortgage payoff ($) and Seller closing costs ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate net . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
