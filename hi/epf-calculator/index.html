About the EPF calculator

Estimate monthly EPF (Provident Fund) employee and employer shares from basic salary. Illustration for India payroll.

How to use the EPF calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Monthly basic salary (₹) and Employee PF rate (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
