About the time card calculator

Calculate total hours worked from clock-in and clock-out times, minus break minutes.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the time card calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in अंदर घड़ी, घड़ी ख़त्म और ब्रेक (मिनट).
  2. Get the result. Press घंटों काम किया. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this time card calculator free?

Yes. The time card calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
