About the voltage divider

Calculate output voltage of a resistive voltage divider. Free electronics calculator.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the voltage divider

  1. Enter your details. Fill in इनपुट वोल्टेज विन (वी), R1 (Ω) शीर्ष और R2 (Ω) तल.
  2. Get the result. Press वाउट. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this voltage divider free?

Yes. The voltage divider is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
