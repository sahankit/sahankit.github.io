New vs old tax regime breakeven (FY 2026–27)

का प्रयोग करें ₹12.75 LPA preset (nil tax in the new regime after ₹75,000 standard deduction) or the full income tax calculator. Pair with the एचआरए कैलकुलेटर if you pay rent.

Rule of thumb: low deductions (little HRA or 80C) usually favour the new regime. Large metro HRA plus full ₹1.5 lakh 80C often favours the old regime. There is no single salary cutoff—run both columns with your numbers.
