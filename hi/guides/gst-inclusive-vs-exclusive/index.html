Inclusive vs exclusive GST formulas

Exclusive (add GST): GST = amount × rate; total = amount + GST. Example: ₹1,000 at 18% → GST ₹180, total ₹1,180.

Inclusive (remove GST): base = total ÷ (1 + rate); GST = total − base. Example: ₹1,180 including 18% → base ₹1,000, GST ₹180.
