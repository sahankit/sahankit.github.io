About the ohm's law

Calculate voltage, current, or resistance using Ohm's law (V = I × R). Free electronics calculator.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the ohm's law

  1. Enter your details. Fill in वोल्टेज (वी), वर्तमान (ए) और प्रतिरोध (Ω).
  2. Get the result. Press गणना करें. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this ohm's law free?

Yes. The ohm's law is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
