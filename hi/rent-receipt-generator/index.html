About the rent receipt

Fill landlord, tenant, and rent details. Generate one receipt per month and print for HRA records—browser-only template.

How to use the rent receipt

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Tenant name , Landlord name , Property address , Rent amount per month (₹) and From month .
  2. Get the result. Press Preview receipt(s) . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. आउटपुट कॉपी या डाउनलोड करें। कुछ सेव नहीं होता—रीफ़्रेश करके फिर शुरू करें।
