About the coast FIRE calculator

See whether invested assets are enough to grow to your FIRE number without more contributions, or how many years of saving remain at your current rate.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the coast FIRE calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in सेवानिवृत्ति में वार्षिक खर्च ($), सुरक्षित निकासी दर (%), आज निवेशित संपत्ति ($), मासिक बचत ($) और अपेक्षित वार्षिक रिटर्न (%).
  2. Get the result. Press तट पर आग की जाँच करें. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this coast FIRE calculator free?

Yes. The coast FIRE calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
