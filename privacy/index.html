Privacy policy

Short version: tools process your input in your browser, and this site shows ads from Google, which may use cookies.

Data you enter in tools

Text, numbers, and files you use in the tools are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to or stored by this website. Some tools load open-source libraries from a public CDN (jsDelivr) to do this work; the libraries run on your device.

A few tools need an outside service: the domain age checker queries public RDAP servers (rdap.org), the IP lookup asks ipify.org for your public IP, and the news brief may call rss2json.com when you tap Refresh (otherwise it uses a snapshot file on this site). Those requests go from your browser directly to that service.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.

Local storage

A few tools (for example the resume builder) can save drafts in your browser's local storage so you don't lose work. That data stays on your device; clear it any time from your browser settings.

Accounts

The tools need no account and collect no names or email addresses. Y-Chat is a separate chat app with its own terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
