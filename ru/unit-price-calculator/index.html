About the unit price calculator

Compare price per unit for two package sizes to see which deal is cheaper per ounce, liter, or custom unit.

How to use the unit price calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Price ($) and Quantity (units) .
  2. Get the result. Press Compare . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Скопируйте или скачайте результат. Ничего не сохраняется—обновите страницу, чтобы начать заново.
