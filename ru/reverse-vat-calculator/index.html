About the reverse VAT calculator

Remove VAT from a gross (VAT-inclusive) price. EU reverse VAT calculator.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the reverse VAT calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Сумма брутто, вкл. НДС (€) и Ставка НДС (%).
  2. Get the result. Press Удалить НДС. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
