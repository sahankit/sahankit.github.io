Free income tax calculator for salaried employees in India

Use this page to estimate how much income tax you may pay under the new tax regime (section 115BAC) versus the old regime for financial year 2026–27 (assessment year 2027–28). Enter your gross salary and deductions such as 80C investments, HRA, and 80D medical insurance. The tool shows side-by-side tax, health and education cess, annual take-home, and monthly in-hand so you can decide which regime is better for you before talking to your employer or chartered accountant.

Everything runs in your browser. We do not store salary or PAN data on a server.

New tax regime vs old tax regime — quick comparison

The new regime is the default for many salaried taxpayers but you can opt for the old regime if it reduces your tax. The new regime allows a higher standard deduction on salary (₹75,000) and simpler slabs, but most deductions under Chapter VI-A (including 80C and HRA) are not available. The old regime uses a ₹50,000 standard deduction and the traditional slabs, but you can claim 80C, HRA, 80D, extra NPS, and other eligible deductions.

  • Новый режим — Often better when you have few deductions (no or low HRA, minimal 80C/80D). Salaried taxpayers may pay nil tax when taxable income (after the ₹75,000 standard deduction) is up to ₹12 lakh via section 87A, subject to current law.
  • Старый режим — Often better when HRA exemption is large (metros), you use full 80C, pay health premiums (80D), or have substantial housing loan interest and other VI-A deductions.
  • Which is advantageous? — There is no fixed cutoff salary: run this calculator with your real deduction numbers. A ₹12 lakh package with high HRA may favour the old regime; the same salary with zero HRA may favour the new regime.

FY 2026–27 income tax slab rates (reference)

Slab rates used in this calculator for resident individuals below 60 years:

  • Новый режим (on taxable income): 0–₹4L nil; ₹4–8L 5%; ₹8–12L 10%; ₹12–16L 15%; ₹16–20L 20%; ₹20–24L 25%; above ₹24L 30%.
  • Старый режим (on taxable income): 0–₹2.5L nil; ₹2.5–5L 5%; ₹5–10L 20%; above ₹10L 30%.
  • Health and education cess: 4% on tax after rebate. Section 87A rebate applied where eligible (new: taxable up to ₹12L; old: taxable up to ₹5L).

Deductions you can model (old regime only)

For the old regime column, include eligible amounts for 80C/80CCC/80CCD(1) combined (capped at ₹1.5 lakh in this tool), additional NPS under 80CCD(1B) (up to ₹50,000), 80D medical insurance, HRA exemption under section 10(13A), and a lump sum for other VI-A items (80E education loan interest, 80G donations, etc.). Home loan interest under section 24(b) can be added in “other” if you know the annual eligible amount.

Частые вопросы

Что лучше — новый налоговый режим или старый налоговый режим в Индии?

Это зависит от вашего валового дохода и отчислений. Низкие отчисления обычно благоприятствуют новому режиму; высокий HRA, 80C, 80D и проценты на жилье обычно благоприятствуют старому режиму. Используйте этот калькулятор с фактическими цифрами, чтобы увидеть расчетный налог по обоим пунктам.

What is the income tax slab for FY 2026-27 new regime?

This tool uses FY 2026–27 new regime slabs (Budget 2025 update): nil up to ₹4 lakh taxable, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, and 30% on higher bands, plus ₹75,000 standard deduction on salary and section 87A when taxable income is up to ₹12 lakh.

Могу ли я претендовать на 80C в новом налоговом режиме?

Нет. Большинство вычетов по Главе VI-A, включая 80C, недоступны в новом налоговом режиме. Введите 80C только для расчета по старому режиму.

Предусмотрено ли освобождение от HRA в новом налоговом режиме?

Нет. HRA согласно разделу 10(13A) применяется только в старом режиме. Введите свое ежегодное освобождение от HRA в разделе отчислений по старому режиму.

Why is tax zero when salary is around ₹12.75 lakh in the new regime?

Section 87A (rebate under the Income-tax Act, 2025, often searched as section 156) can rebate tax to nil when taxable income after the ₹75,000 salary standard deduction does not exceed ₹12 lakh. A gross salary of about ₹12.75 lakh can therefore show nil tax. Just above ₹12 lakh taxable, marginal relief caps extra tax at the rupees by which you crossed that limit (before 4% cess).

Могут ли наемные работники каждый год переключаться между режимами?

Наемные работники, как правило, могут выбирать режим каждый финансовый год посредством подачи декларации работодателя или налоговой декларации в соответствии с действующими правилами подоходного налога. У налогоплательщиков-предпринимателей действуют другие правила блокировки.

Is section 87A the same as section 156 of the Income-tax Act, 2025?

Searchers still use the familiar 87A / 115BAC / 80C numbers. The 2025 Act recasts the rebate (often cited as section 156) and the new-regime default. This page names both so FY 2026–27 (tax year 2026–27) queries match.

Включает ли этот калькулятор 4%-ный сбор?

Да. Налог на здравоохранение и образование в размере 4% добавляется к налогу после скидки по разделу 87A для обоих режимов.
