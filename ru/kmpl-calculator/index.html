About the KMPL calculator

Calculate kilometers per litre from distance driven and fuel used. Free KMPL calculator for cars and bikes.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the KMPL calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Расстояние (км) и Израсходованное топливо (литры).
  2. Get the result. Press Рассчитать. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this KMPL calculator free?

Yes. The KMPL calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
