DPI from a photo

DPI is pixels divided by inches. A 1200-pixel-wide image printed 4 inches wide is 300 DPI. The same image printed 10 inches wide is 120 DPI and will look soft up close. The calculator also shows the width in millimetres.

How to use the DPI calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Ширина (пикселей), Ширина (дюймы) и DPI.
  2. Get the result. Press DPI в пикселях и дюймах. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

DPI — это то же самое, что PPI?

За эту сумму да. PPI — это количество пикселей на дюйм экрана или файла. DPI — это соответствующий термин печати. Число в любом случае составляет пиксели ÷ дюймы.

Is this DPI calculator free?

Yes. The DPI calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
