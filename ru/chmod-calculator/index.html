How to use the chmod calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Числовой (например, 755) и Символический.
  2. Get the result. Press Число → rwx. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
