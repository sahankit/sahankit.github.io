How to use the personal loan EMI

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Сумма кредита (₹), Процентная ставка (% в год) и Срок владения (месяцев).
  2. Get the result. Press Рассчитать EMI. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
