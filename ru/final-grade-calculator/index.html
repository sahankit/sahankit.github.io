About the final grade calculator

Compute your course grade from weighted categories such as homework, midterms, and the final exam.

Работает в браузере—без регистрации и установки.

How to use the final grade calculator

  1. Enter your details. Compute your course grade from weighted categories such as homework, midterms, and the final exam.
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate grade . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Скопируйте или скачайте результат. Ничего не сохраняется—обновите страницу, чтобы начать заново.
