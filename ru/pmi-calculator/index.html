Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the PMI calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Цена дома ($), Первоначальный взнос ($), Ставка по ипотеке (%), Срок (лет) и Ставка PMI (% кредита/год).
  2. Get the result. Press Оценить PMI. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Yes. The PMI calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.
