What the button does

Each click takes one random bit. Yes and no each have an even chance, and the previous answer does not affect the next one. Use it as a coin flip when you do not mind either result.

How to use the yes or no

  1. Enter your details. Get a random yes or no. Each click is a fresh coin flip in your browser. It is a decision toy, not advice.
  2. Get the result. Press Ask . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Sao chép hoặc tải kết quả. Không lưu—tải lại trang để làm lại.

Is it fair?

Yes. It uses the browser’s cryptographic random numbers, and the answer is whichever side that bit lands on.
