Peptide reconstitution calculator

Peptide reconstitution math: concentration after adding bacteriostatic water and volume to draw for a dose in micrograms. For education only, not medical advice.

Concentration = (mg × 1000) ÷ ml mcg per ml. Volume = dose ÷ concentration. Confirm doses with a qualified clinician.

How to use the peptide calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Peptide in vial (mg) , Bacteriostatic water added (ml) and Desired dose (mcg) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Sao chép hoặc tải kết quả. Không lưu—tải lại trang để làm lại.
