How to use the PPF calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Annual deposit (₹) , Interest rate (% p.a.) and Years .
  2. Get the result. Press Maturity . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Sao chép hoặc tải kết quả. Không lưu—tải lại trang để làm lại.

When to use the PPF calculator

Estimate Public Provident Fund maturity with fixed annual deposit (simplified). Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
