About the advance tax

Split estimated annual tax into four advance tax installments (25% each). Illustration—not filing software.

How to use the advance tax

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Estimated annual tax (₹) .
  2. Get the result. Press Split . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Sao chép hoặc tải kết quả. Không lưu—tải lại trang để làm lại.
