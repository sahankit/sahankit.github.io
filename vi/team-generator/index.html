How teams are filled
Names are shuffled, then dealt one by one across the teams, like cards. If the list does not divide evenly, some teams have one extra person. Running it again makes a new shuffle.
How to use the random team generator
  1. Enter your details. Fill in Names, one per line and Number of teams .
  2. Get the result. Press Make teams . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Sao chép hoặc tải kết quả. Không lưu—tải lại trang để làm lại.
