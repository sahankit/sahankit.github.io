How teams are filled
Names are shuffled, then dealt one by one across the teams, like cards. If the list does not divide evenly, some teams have one extra person. Running it again makes a new shuffle.
How to use the random team generator
  1. Enter your details. Fill in Names, one per line and Number of teams.
  2. Get the result. Press Make teams. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
Frequently asked questions
Can two teams end up the same?
Only by chance. Each click shuffles again.
Is this random team generator free?
Yes. The random team generator is free to use in your browser with no account required.
Does my data leave my device?
For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
