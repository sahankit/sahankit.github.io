How to use the BTU calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Room area (sq ft) and Sun exposure.
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate BTU. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Yes. The BTU calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.
