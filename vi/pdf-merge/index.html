How to use the merge PDF

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Merge & download . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Sao chép hoặc tải kết quả. Không lưu—tải lại trang để làm lại.

When to use the merge PDF

Combine multiple PDF files into one in the browser. Private merge with pdf-lib. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
