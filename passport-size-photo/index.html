Common passport photo sizes

Rules change and vary by application, so always check the latest official guidelines before submitting.

  • India passport: 35 × 45 mm (3.5 × 4.5 cm), white background, face covering most of the frame
  • US passport and visa, India visa/OCI: 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm)
  • UK, Schengen/EU, Australia: 35 × 45 mm
  • PAN card / stamp size: 25 × 35 mm (2.5 × 3.5 cm)
  • Canada passport: 50 × 70 mm

How to make and print your photo

  • Take a photo facing the camera in good light against a plain white or light wall.
  • Upload it, drag to centre your face and use Zoom so the head fits between the guide lines.
  • Download the single photo (saved at 300 dpi) for online applications.
  • Or download a print sheet—up to 8 photos on 4 × 6 inch paper—and print it at any photo shop at 100% size.

Frequently asked questions

Can it change my background to white?

No. Use a plain light background when taking the photo; the tool crops, resizes and lays out the photo but does not remove backgrounds.

How do I print at the right size?

Print the sheet on 4 × 6 inch photo paper at 100% / actual size. Do not choose “fit to page”.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. All editing happens in your browser.

Is this passport size photo free?

Yes. The passport size photo is free to use in your browser with no account required.
