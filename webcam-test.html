How to test your webcam

Press Test my webcam and allow camera access when your browser asks. If the preview appears, your camera works. The tool then shows the resolution it is streaming (for example 1280 × 720 or 1920 × 1080), the measured frame rate, aspect ratio and megapixels.

Have more than one camera? Pick it from the list. Use Take snapshot to save a still image to check focus and lighting before a video call.

Webcam not working? Try this

  • Allow camera permission for this site from the camera icon in the address bar.
  • Close Zoom, Teams, Meet or other apps that may be using the camera.
  • On Windows, check Settings → Privacy → Camera. On macOS, check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  • Check for a physical privacy shutter or a camera function key on laptops.

How to use the webcam test

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Camera.
  2. Get the result. Press Test my webcam. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video recorded or uploaded?

No. The video stays on your device. Snapshots are created locally and only saved if you download them.

Why is the resolution lower than my camera’s spec?

Browsers may choose a lower mode for performance, and some USB ports or apps limit it. The value shown is what your camera is actually streaming.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, on modern mobile browsers. Use the camera list to switch between front and back cameras.

Is this webcam test free?

Yes. The webcam test is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
