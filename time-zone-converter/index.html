    How to convert time zones

    Pick a date and time, choose the time zone it is in (your local zone is selected automatically), and see the matching time in every zone in your list. Add cities with + Add and remove them with ×. Your list is saved for next time.

    Daylight saving time is applied automatically for the selected date, so conversions to New York, London or Sydney stay correct all year.

    Popular conversions from India (IST, UTC+05:30)

    • IST is 10 h 30 min ahead of New York in winter (EST) and 9 h 30 min ahead in summer (EDT).
    • IST is 13 h 30 min ahead of California in winter (PST) and 12 h 30 min ahead in summer (PDT).
    • IST is 5 h 30 min ahead of London in winter (GMT) and 4 h 30 min ahead in summer (BST).
    • IST is 3 h 30 min behind Japan (JST) and 2 h 30 min behind Singapore (SGT).

    How to use the time zone converter

    1. Enter your details. Fill in Date and time, From time zone and Compare with.
    2. Get the result. Press Convert. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
    3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does it handle daylight saving time?

    Yes. It uses your browser’s time zone database, which includes current and historic daylight saving rules.

    What is IST in UTC?

    Indian Standard Time is UTC+05:30 all year; India does not use daylight saving.

    Can I convert a future date?

    Yes. Pick any date—the offsets for that date are used.

    Is this time zone converter free?

    Yes. The time zone converter is free to use in your browser with no account required.

    Does my data leave my device?

    For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
