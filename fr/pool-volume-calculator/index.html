About the pool volume

Calculate swimming pool volume in gallons from length, width, and average depth. Free pool calculator.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the pool volume

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Longueur (pi), Largeur (pi) et Profondeur moyenne (pi).
  2. Get the result. Press Calculer les gallons. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Yes. The pool volume is free to use in your browser with no account required.
