How to use the UTM builder

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Page URL , utm_source , utm_medium and utm_campaign .
  2. Get the result. Press Build . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.

When to use the UTM builder

Build URLs with utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign for analytics. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
