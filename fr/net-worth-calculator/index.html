About the net worth calculator

Calculate personal net worth from assets and debts. US household net worth snapshot.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the net worth calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Argent et épargne ($), Placements et retraite ($), Valeur de la maison ($), Autres actifs ($) et Hypothèque et prêts ($).
  2. Get the result. Press Calculer. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this net worth calculator free?

Yes. The net worth calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
