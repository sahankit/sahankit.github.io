How to use the mask → CIDR

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Masque de sous-réseau.
  2. Get the result. Press Convertir. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
