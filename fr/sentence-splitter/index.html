How to use the sentence splitter

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Language .
  2. Get the result. Press Split . Results update on the page.

When to use the sentence splitter

Split paragraphs into sentences using the browser Intl Segmenter. Free, works in Chrome, Safari, and Firefox. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
