How to use the fuel efficiency

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Distance (km) and Fuel used (litres) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.

When to use the fuel efficiency

Calculate km per litre from distance and fuel used. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
