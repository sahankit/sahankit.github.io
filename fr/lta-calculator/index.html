Calculateur LTA

Estimate how much of your Leave Travel Allowance can be exempt from tax when travel costs and LTA received are known. Uses common salary-tax assumptions.

L'exemption LTA est généralement le montant le plus faible entre l'allocation reçue et le coût du voyage en Inde pour vous et votre famille sur les itinéraires autorisés. Vous pouvez réclamer jusqu’à deux voyages par bloc de quatre années civiles. Conservez les tickets et les preuves d’employeur.

Estimate how much of your Leave Travel Allowance can be exempt from tax when travel costs and LTA received are known. Uses common salary-tax assumptions.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the LTA calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in LTA reçu de l'employeur ( ₹ / an), Frais de voyage éligibles réels ( ₹ ) et Les voyages ont réclamé ce bloc (max 2).
  2. Get the result. Press Exonération d’estimation. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this LTA calculator free?

Yes. The LTA calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
