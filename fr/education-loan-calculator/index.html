How to use the education loan EMI

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Loan amount (₹) , Interest (% p.a.) and Tenure (months) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate EMI . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
