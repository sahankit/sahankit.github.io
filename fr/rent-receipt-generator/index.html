About the rent receipt

Fill landlord, tenant, and rent details. Generate one receipt per month and print for HRA records—browser-only template.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the rent receipt

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Nom du locataire, Nom du propriétaire, Adresse de la propriété, Montant du loyer par mois ( ₹ ) et Du mois.
  2. Get the result. Press Aperçu des reçus. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this rent receipt free?

Yes. The rent receipt is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
