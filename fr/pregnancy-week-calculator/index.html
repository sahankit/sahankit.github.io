About the pregnancy weeks

Calculate pregnancy week and day from the first day of your last period (LMP). Estimate only.

Fonctionne dans le navigateur—sans compte ni installation.

How to use the pregnancy weeks

  1. Enter your details. Fill in First day of last period (LMP) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
