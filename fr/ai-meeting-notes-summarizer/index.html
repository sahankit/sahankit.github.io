Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI and enough free disk space. Text is processed on your device—not on Sahankit servers.

How to use the meeting notes AI

  1. Enter your details. Summarize meeting notes into key points and action items with Chrome Summarizer.
  2. Get the result. Press Summarize notes . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
