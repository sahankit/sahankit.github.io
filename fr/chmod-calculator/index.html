How to use the chmod calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Numeric (e.g. 755) and Symbolic .
  2. Get the result. Press Num → rwx . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.

When to use the chmod calculator

Convert between chmod numeric (755) and rwx permissions. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.
