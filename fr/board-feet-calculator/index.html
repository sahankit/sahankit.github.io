About the board feet

Calculate board feet from thickness, width, and length in inches. Free lumber calculator.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the board feet

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Épaisseur (po), Largeur (po), Longueur (pi) et Nombre de pièces.
  2. Get the result. Press Pieds de planche. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this board feet free?

Yes. The board feet is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
