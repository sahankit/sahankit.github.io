About the BP category

See blood pressure category from systolic and diastolic readings. Educational—ask a clinician for health advice.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the BP category

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Systolique (mmHg) et Diastolique (mmHg).
  2. Get the result. Press Vérifier. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this BP category free?

Yes. The BP category is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
