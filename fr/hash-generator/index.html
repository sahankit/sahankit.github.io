When to use SHA-256

SHA-256 produces a fixed-length fingerprint of text—handy for checksums, API signatures, and learning how hashing works. It is not encryption; do not hash passwords for login systems without proper salting and slow algorithms.

How to use the SHA-256 hash

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Text and SHA-256 (hex) .
  2. Get the result. Press Hash . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
