About the CTC to in-hand

Rough in-hand salary from annual CTC with basic, HRA, and deduction percent. Planning illustration only.

How to use the CTC to in-hand

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Annual CTC (₹) and Total deductions (% of CTC) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
