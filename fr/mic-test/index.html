How to test your microphone

Press Tester mon micro and allow access. Speak normally—the level bar and waveform should move. A level around −30 to −10 dBFS while talking is healthy; constantly near 0 means the input is too loud and may distort.

Press Enregistrez 5 secondes to capture a short clip and play it back to hear how you sound on calls. Echo cancellation and noise suppression are turned off so you hear the raw microphone.

Microphone not detected?

  • Choose the correct device from the Microphone list (headset vs. built-in).
  • Check mute switches on headsets and the system input volume.
  • Allow microphone permission for this site in the address bar.
  • Close other apps that may be holding the microphone, then retry.

How to use the mic test

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Micro.
  2. Get the result. Press Tester mon micro. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Ma voix est-elle téléchargée ?

Non. L’audio est analysé et enregistré uniquement dans votre navigateur et est supprimé lorsque vous quittez la page.

Que signifie dBFS ?

Décibels par rapport à la pleine échelle. 0 dBFS est le signal le plus fort possible ; les sons plus faibles sont des nombres négatifs.

Puis-je tester des AirPods ou des casques Bluetooth ?

Oui. Connectez-les d'abord, puis sélectionnez-les dans la liste Microphone après avoir accordé l'autorisation.

Is this mic test free?

Yes. The mic test is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
