The rule

A year is a leap year when it divides by 4. If it also divides by 100, it is not a leap year, unless it divides by 400. 2024 and 2028 are leap years. 2100 is not.

How to use the leap year checker

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Année.
  2. Get the result. Press Vérifier l'année. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

L'année 2000 était-elle une année bissextile ?

Oui. 2000 divise par 400, donc l’exception du siècle ne s’applique pas.

Is this leap year checker free?

Yes. The leap year checker is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?
