What is included

Every Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday from the start date through the end date is counted. A range that is only a Saturday and Sunday is zero weekdays. National and state holidays still fall on weekdays, so subtract those if your count should exclude them.

How to use the business days calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Start date and End date .
  2. Get the result. Press Count weekdays . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.

When to use the business days calculator

Count Monday-to-Friday days between two dates. Weekends are skipped. Public holidays are not, unless you subtract them yourself. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
