What is included

Every Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday from the start date through the end date is counted. A range that is only a Saturday and Sunday is zero weekdays. National and state holidays still fall on weekdays, so subtract those if your count should exclude them.

How to use the business days calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Date de début et Date de fin.
  2. Get the result. Press Compter les jours de la semaine. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Est-ce qu'il connaît les fêtes indiennes ?

Non. Les listes de jours fériés changent selon l’État et l’employeur. Soustrayez ces dates du total de la semaine.

Is this business days calculator free?

Yes. The business days calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
