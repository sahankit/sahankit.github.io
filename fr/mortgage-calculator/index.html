How to use the mortgage calculator (PITI)

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Home price ($) , Down payment ($) , Interest rate (% APR) , Loan term (years) and Property tax ($ / year) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate PITI . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
