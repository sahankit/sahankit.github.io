About the TDEE calculator

Calculate total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) and BMR using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Free calorie calculator.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the TDEE calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Sexe, Âge (années), Poids (kg), Hauteur (cm) et Activité.
  2. Get the result. Press Calculer. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this TDEE calculator free?

Yes. The TDEE calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
