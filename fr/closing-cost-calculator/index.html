About the closing cost calculator

Rough estimate of US home buyer closing costs as a percent of purchase price plus optional lender points.

Fonctionne dans le navigateur—sans compte ni installation.

How to use the closing cost calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Purchase price ($) , Closing costs (% of price) and Lender points ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
