About the days between dates

Comptez les jours, les semaines et les mois entre deux dates du calendrier.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the days between dates

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Date de début et Date de fin.
  2. Get the result. Press Compter les jours. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this days between dates free?

Yes. The days between dates is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
