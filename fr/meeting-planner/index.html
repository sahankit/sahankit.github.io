How to use the meeting time zones

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Votre date et heure locales et Comparer les zones.
  2. Get the result. Press Comparez. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this meeting time zones free?

Yes. The meeting time zones is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
