How to use the social post AI

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Plateforme et Sujet ou annonce.
  2. Get the result. Press Écrire un message. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this social post AI free?

Yes. The social post AI is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
