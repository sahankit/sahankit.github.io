About the professional tax

Illustrative monthly professional tax from gross salary using simplified Karnataka-style slabs. Not official for all states.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the professional tax

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Salaire mensuel brut ( ₹ ).
  2. Get the result. Press Estimation du TP. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this professional tax free?

Yes. The professional tax is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
