About the professional tax

Illustrative monthly professional tax from gross salary using simplified Karnataka-style slabs. Not official for all states.

Fonctionne dans le navigateur—sans compte ni installation.

How to use the professional tax

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Gross monthly salary (₹) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate PT . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
