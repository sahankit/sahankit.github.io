About the emergency fund

Calculate emergency fund target from monthly expenses and months of coverage. Free savings goal.

Fonctionne dans le navigateur—sans compte ni installation.

How to use the emergency fund

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Monthly expenses ($) and Months to cover .
  2. Get the result. Press Target fund . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.
