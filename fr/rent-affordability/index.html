How to use the rent affordability

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Monthly gross income and Max % for housing .
  2. Get the result. Press Max rent . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copiez ou téléchargez le résultat. Rien n’est enregistré—actualisez pour recommencer.

When to use the rent affordability

Suggested max rent from monthly income using the 30% guideline. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
