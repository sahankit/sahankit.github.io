How to use the pregnancy weight gain

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Hauteur (cm) et Poids avant la grossesse (kg).
  2. Get the result. Press Afficher la gamme. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
