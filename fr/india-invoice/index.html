Frequently asked questions

Est-ce juridiquement valable ?

Utilisez votre autorité de certification et votre logiciel de facturation officiel pour vous conformer ; il s'agit d'une mise en page de démarrage.

Is this GST invoice free?

Yes. The GST invoice is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
