About the DMARC generator

Generate a DMARC TXT record for _dmarc.yourdomain. Free DNS email authentication template.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the DMARC generator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Politique (p=), Courriel de rapports agrégés (rua) et Pourcentage (pct).
  2. Get the result. Press Générer. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this DMARC generator free?

Yes. The DMARC generator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
