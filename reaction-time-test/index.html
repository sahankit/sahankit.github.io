Average human reaction time

Most people react to a visual cue in about 200–300 milliseconds, with a typical average near 250 ms. Under 200 ms is exceptional. Results also include the delay of your mouse, screen and browser, so a high-refresh monitor and wired mouse give slightly faster readings.

How the test works

Click the box to start. It turns red and waits a random 1–4.5 seconds, then turns green. Click as soon as you see green. Clicking early counts as “too soon” and is not scored. After five valid tries you get your average.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes. Focus the box and press Space or Enter instead of clicking.

Why are my results slower on a phone?

Touchscreens and phone browsers add some input latency. Compare scores on the same device.

Does sleep or caffeine affect reaction time?

Yes. Fatigue slows reactions noticeably; caffeine can improve them slightly. Test at different times to see.

Is this reaction time test free?

Yes. The reaction time test is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?
