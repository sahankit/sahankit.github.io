How to crop an image

  • Choose a photo (JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF).
  • Pick an aspect ratio—Free, 1:1 square, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails, 4:5 for Instagram portrait, or 9:16 for stories and reels.
  • Drag inside the box to move it and drag a corner to resize. Arrow keys nudge it (hold Shift for bigger steps).
  • Choose PNG, JPG or WebP and press Download cropped image.

Full quality, completely private

Cropping happens at the image’s original resolution, so you do not lose detail. The crop size in pixels is shown as you drag. The file is processed in your browser and never uploaded.

How to use the crop image

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Download cropped image. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Which ratio should I use for Instagram?

1:1 for square posts, 4:5 for portrait posts (the largest feed size), and 9:16 for stories and reels.

Will cropping reduce quality?

No. PNG is lossless; JPG and WebP are saved at high quality (92%).

Can I crop on my phone?

Yes. Drag with your finger to move or resize the crop box.

Is this crop image free?

Yes. The crop image is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
