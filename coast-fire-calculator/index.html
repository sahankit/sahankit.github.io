What Coast FIRE means

Coast FIRE is the point where money already invested is enough to grow to your retirement target if you stop adding new savings. You still cover living costs from work, but you do not need to keep investing aggressively.

This calculator uses a simple future-value model with a safe withdrawal rate (often 4%) and your expected return.

How to use the coast FIRE calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Annual spending in retirement ($), Safe withdrawal rate (%), Invested assets today ($), Monthly savings ($) and Expected annual return (%).
  2. Get the result. Press Check coast FIRE. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
