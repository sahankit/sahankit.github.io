Video to MP3 converter

Convert a local video file to MP3 in your browser. Private WebAssembly conversion—no upload, no account.

Pick a video file on your device (MP4, WebM, MOV, etc.). Conversion runs in your browser with WebAssembly—nothing is uploaded. Does not accept YouTube or reel links; download those with official apps first if allowed.

First run downloads ~25 MB WASM encoder (one time per browser). Large videos need free RAM.

Private video to MP3 conversion

This tool is for files you already have on your device. ffmpeg runs as WebAssembly inside Chrome, Edge, or Firefox—Sahankit does not receive your video.

It does not download from YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok URLs. Use platform-approved methods to obtain video first, then convert here if you have the rights.

Frequently asked questions

Is my video uploaded?

No. Encoding happens locally after you pick a file.

Why is the first conversion slow?

The browser downloads the ffmpeg WASM bundle once (about 25 MB).

Can I convert YouTube to MP3?

Not here. Paste links do not work—only local files from the file picker.

Is this video to MP3 free?

Yes. The video to MP3 is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
