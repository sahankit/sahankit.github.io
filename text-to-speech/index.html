Listen to any text

Paste an article, notes or an email and press Speak. Choose a voice to change the accent or language—Hindi, English (India, US, UK) and many other voices are available depending on your device. Slow the speed down for learning or speed it up to skim.

Long text is split into sentences so it keeps playing smoothly. Use Pause, Resume and Stop at any time.

Which voices will I see?

The tool uses the Web Speech API, which plays voices installed on your device or provided by your browser. Chrome and Edge include extra online voices; Safari uses Apple system voices; Windows and Android voices can be added in system settings.

How to use the text to speech

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Text to read aloud, Voice, Speed and Pitch.
  2. Get the result. Press Speak. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download the speech as MP3?

Not with this tool—browsers do not allow saving Web Speech output. You can record it with screen or audio recording software.

Does it support Hindi?

Yes, if a Hindi voice is available on your device. Look for “hi-IN” in the voice list.

Is my text sent to a server?

Text is processed by your browser’s speech engine. Some browser-provided online voices may send text to that browser’s speech service.

Is this text to speech free?

Yes. The text to speech is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
