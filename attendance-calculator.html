How the attendance formula works

Attendance % = classes attended ÷ classes held × 100. For example, 28 of 40 classes is 70%.

To reach a required percentage r, you need to attend x more classes in a row so that (attended + x) ÷ (held + x) ≥ r, which gives x = (r × held − attended) ÷ (1 − r), rounded up. With 28 of 40 and a 75% rule, you need 8 more classes: 36 ÷ 48 = 75%.

If you’re above the requirement, you can miss y classes while attended ÷ (held + y) ≥ r, so y = attended ÷ r − held, rounded down.

Why 75% matters

Many Indian universities and colleges, including those following UGC and AICTE norms, require at least 75% attendance to sit end-semester exams, with limited condonation for medical reasons. Rules vary by institution—check your college’s handbook.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count labs and lectures separately?

Enter whichever your college counts. If labs and theory are tracked separately, calculate each one.

Is 74.5% rounded to 75%?

That depends on your institution. To be safe, aim to stay at or above the exact requirement.

Can I plan future classes?

Yes—open “Plan ahead” and enter upcoming classes and how many you’ll attend.

Is this attendance calculator free?

Yes. The attendance calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
