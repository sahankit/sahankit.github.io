How Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana works

  • Open for a girl child aged up to 10, at a post office or authorised bank.
  • Deposit between ₹250 and ₹1,50,000 each financial year for 15 years.
  • The account matures 21 years after opening; interest keeps compounding after deposits stop.
  • Up to 50% can be withdrawn after the girl turns 18 for higher education.
  • Deposits qualify for Section 80C (old regime), and interest and maturity are tax-free (EEE).

How the maturity value is calculated

This calculator assumes the full yearly deposit is made at the start of each year and interest is compounded annually at the rate you enter. Example: ₹1,50,000 a year at 8.2% gives total deposits of ₹22,50,000 and a maturity value of about ₹71.8 lakh after 21 years.

In the actual scheme, interest is calculated monthly on the lowest balance between the 5th and the end of the month, so deposit before the 5th of April each year to earn the most.

How to use the sukanya Samriddhi (SSY)

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Yearly deposit (₹250 to ₹1,50,000), Interest rate (% per year), Girl's age at account opening (0–10 years) and Account opening year.
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate maturity. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current SSY interest rate?

The government sets it quarterly; it has been 8.2% per year in recent quarters. Enter the latest rate in the calculator.

Is the SSY maturity amount taxable?

No. SSY has EEE status: deposits (under 80C, old regime), interest and maturity are all tax-free.

What if I miss a yearly deposit?

The account becomes inactive. It can be revived by paying the minimum ₹250 for each missed year plus a ₹50 penalty per year.

Is this sukanya Samriddhi (SSY) free?

Yes. The sukanya Samriddhi (SSY) is free to use in your browser with no account required.
