About the old vs new tax regime

Compare estimated income tax under India's old and new personal tax regimes for FY 2026-27. Includes standard deduction, 87A rebate, and common 80C/HRA inputs for the old regime.

How to use the old vs new tax regime

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Gross salary (₹ / year) , 80C + 80CCD(1B) etc. (₹, old regime) and HRA + other exemptions (₹, old regime) .
