About the SPF generator

Build a basic SPF TXT record for email domains. Copy-paste DNS helper—not a mail server setup tool.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the SPF generator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 도메인 그리고 추가 IPv4(선택 사항).
  2. Get the result. Press TXT 생성. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this SPF generator free?

Yes. The SPF generator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
