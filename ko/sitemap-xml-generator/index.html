About the sitemap generator

Generate a basic sitemap.xml from a list of page URLs. Free XML sitemap builder for small sites.

How to use the sitemap generator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Site base URL and Page paths or full URLs (one per line) .
  2. Get the result. Press Generate sitemap.xml . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. 결과를 복사하거나 다운로드하세요. 저장되지 않습니다. 새로고침하면 처음부터입니다.
