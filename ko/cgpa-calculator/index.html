CGPA conversion formulas

Schools use different multipliers (9.5× and 10× are common). Confirm with your institution.

How to use the CGPA to %

  1. Enter your details. Fill in CGPA (0–10) , Formula and Multiplier .
  2. Get the result. Press Convert . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 결과를 복사하거나 다운로드하세요. 저장되지 않습니다. 새로고침하면 처음부터입니다.
