How to use the electricity estimate

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 단위(kWh), 단위당 요금(₹) 그리고 고정 요금(₹).
  2. Get the result. Press 견적. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
