About the net worth calculator

Calculate personal net worth from assets and debts. US household net worth snapshot.

How to use the net worth calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Cash & savings ($) , Investments & retirement ($) , Home value ($) , Other assets ($) and Mortgage & loans ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. 결과를 복사하거나 다운로드하세요. 저장되지 않습니다. 새로고침하면 처음부터입니다.
