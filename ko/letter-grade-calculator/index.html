About the letter grade calculator

Convert a percentage score to a US letter grade (A–F). Adjust cutoffs if your school uses different breakpoints.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the letter grade calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in 점수(%), 최소(%), B 최소(%), C 최소값(%) 그리고 D 최소값(%).
  2. Get the result. Press 문자 등급 받기. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this letter grade calculator free?

Yes. The letter grade calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
