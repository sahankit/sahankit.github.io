How to use the weighted average calculator

  1. Enter your details. Calculate a weighted average from scores and percentage weights (weights should total 100).
  2. Get the result. Press 계산하다. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. The weighted average calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
