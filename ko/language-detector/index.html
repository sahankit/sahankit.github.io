    Detect language

    Requires desktop Chrome with built-in AI (Gemini Nano) and enough free disk space. Processing happens on your device.

    How to use the language detector

    1. Enter your details. Detect the language of text with Chrome’s Language Detector API.
    2. Get the result. Press Detect . Results update on the page.
    3. Use or copy it. 결과를 복사하거나 다운로드하세요. 저장되지 않습니다. 새로고침하면 처음부터입니다.
