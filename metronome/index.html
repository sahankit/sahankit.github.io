Rock-steady timing

Clicks are scheduled ahead of time with the Web Audio API, so the beat stays precise even if your device is busy. Choose 30–300 BPM with the slider or +/− buttons, or tap the tempo of a song with Tap tempo. Set beats per bar for 3/4, 4/4, 6/8 and odd meters, add subdivisions, and accent the first beat.

Tempo markings

Practice tip: start slowly enough to play cleanly, then raise the tempo 2–5 BPM at a time.

  • Largo: 40–60 BPM
  • Adagio: 66–76 BPM
  • Andante: 76–108 BPM (walking pace)
  • Moderato: 108–120 BPM
  • Allegro: 120–168 BPM
  • Presto: 168–200 BPM

How to use the metronome

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Tempo, Beats per bar, Subdivision, Sound and Volume.
  2. Get the result. Press Start. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use keyboard shortcuts?

Yes: Space to start or stop, T to tap the tempo, and the left and right arrows to change BPM.

Why can’t I hear anything on iPhone?

Turn off silent mode with the side switch and raise the volume; iOS mutes web audio in silent mode.

Does it keep running in the background?

Usually yes, but some phones pause audio when the screen locks. Keep the screen on during practice.

Is this metronome free?

Yes. The metronome is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
