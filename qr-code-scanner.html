Two ways to scan

Camera: press Scan with camera and point it at the QR code. On phones the rear camera is used. The code is read automatically and the camera stops.

Image: upload a screenshot or photo that contains a QR code—handy for codes received on WhatsApp or email, or when you are on a laptop without a camera.

Scan safely

Links are never opened automatically. Check the decoded address before tapping Open link, and be careful with QR codes that ask for UPI payments or login details.

How to use the QR code scanner

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Scan with camera. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Can I scan a QR code from a screenshot?

Yes. Upload the screenshot using the file picker.

Does it read UPI QR codes?

Yes, it shows the UPI payment link text so you can verify the payee before paying in your UPI app.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Decoding happens in your browser.

Is this QR code scanner free?

Yes. The QR code scanner is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
