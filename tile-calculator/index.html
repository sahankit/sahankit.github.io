About the tile calculator

Calculate how many floor tiles you need from room size and tile dimensions. Adds waste factor.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the tile calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Room length (ft) , Room width (ft) , Tile length (in) , Tile width (in) and Waste (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate tiles . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
