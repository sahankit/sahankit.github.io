How to record your screen

  • Tick whether to include tab/system audio and your microphone.
  • Press Start recording and choose the entire screen, a window or a browser tab.
  • Do what you want to capture—a tutorial, presentation, bug report or game clip.
  • Press Stop recording (or “Stop sharing” in the browser bar), preview the video and download it.

Audio, formats and support

Chrome and Edge can capture audio from a browser tab, and on Windows from the whole system; macOS only allows tab audio. Add your microphone to narrate. Videos are saved as WebM (or MP4 where the browser supports it), which plays in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and VLC and uploads directly to YouTube.

Screen recording requires a desktop browser—mobile browsers don’t allow websites to capture the screen.

How to use the screen recorder

  1. Enter your details. Record your screen, a window or a browser tab online with system audio and microphone. No download, no watermark, no time limit. Save the video to your device.
  2. Get the result. Press Start recording. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a watermark or time limit?

Neither. Recording length is limited only by your device’s memory.

Why is there no sound?

Make sure you ticked “Share tab audio” or “Share system audio” in the browser’s sharing dialog. macOS only supports tab audio.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. The video is created and stored in your browser until you download it.

Is this screen recorder free?

Yes. The screen recorder is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
