Frequently asked questions

How do I compress a photo to exactly 50 KB?

Open the reduce-image tool with a 50 KB target. It adjusts JPEG quality until the file is at or under 50 KB. Crop empty background first if quality drops too low.

Will my photo leave my device?

No. Compression uses canvas in your browser. The image is not sent to ToolHQ servers.

What if the portal also requires pixel dimensions?

On the compressor, expand “Optional: exact size in pixels” and set width × height (e.g. 200×230) before reducing KB.
