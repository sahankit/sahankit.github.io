Frequently asked questions

How do I get a PDF under 200 KB?

Open the PDF compressor, upload your file, and use a lower quality preset. Remove unused pages or merge only needed pages first if still too large.

Is this the same as a government PDF validator?

No. This only changes file size locally. Always check the official portal’s rules for DPI, fonts, and signatures.
