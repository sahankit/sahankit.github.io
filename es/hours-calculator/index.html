About the hours calculator

Add hours and minutes from multiple time entries. Free timesheet hours calculator for work and billing.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the hours calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Hora 1 (horas:minutos), Hora 2 y Hora 3 (opcional).
  2. Get the result. Press Agregar horarios. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this hours calculator free?

Yes. The hours calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
