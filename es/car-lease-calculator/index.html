How to use the car lease calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in MSRP / costo capitalizado ($), Valor residual ($), factor dinero, Plazo (meses) y Pagadero al momento de la firma ($).
  2. Get the result. Press Calcular arrendamiento. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
