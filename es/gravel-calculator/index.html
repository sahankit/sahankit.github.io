How to use the gravel calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Longitud (pies), Ancho (pies) y Profundidad (pulgadas).
  2. Get the result. Press calcular. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
