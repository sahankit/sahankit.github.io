Word a PDF (DOCX)

Turn a Word .docx file into a PDF in your browser. Private conversion with mammoth and html2pdf—no cloud upload.

Los archivos permanecen en su dispositivo. Es posible que los diseños, imágenes y tablas complejos no se transfieran perfectamente; lo mejor para documentos con mucho texto.

Subir .docx sólo (palabra moderna). Legado .doc no es compatible; guárdelo primero como DOCX en Word o Google Docs.

Upload a .docx file. We convert it to HTML with mammoth, then render a PDF with html2pdf.js—all on your device.

Legacy .doc files are not supported; open them in Word and Save As .docx first.

How to use the word to PDF

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Convertir a PDF. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

¿Esto es compatible con .doc?

Sólo .docx (Office Open XML). Guarde los archivos .doc más antiguos como .docx antes de convertirlos.

¿Se conservan las imágenes?

Las imágenes incrustadas en DOCX suelen conservarse; Los diseños muy complejos pueden cambiar ligeramente en el PDF.

Is this word to PDF free?

Yes. The word to PDF is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
