How to use the electricity estimate

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Units (kWh) , Rate per unit (₹) and Fixed charge (₹) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copia o descarga el resultado. No se guarda nada—recarga para empezar de nuevo.

When to use the electricity estimate

Estimate bill from meter units and price per unit. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
