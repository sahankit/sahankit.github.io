How to use the military time

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Horario de 24 horas (HH:MM) y resultado de 12 horas.
  2. Get the result. Press Convertir. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
