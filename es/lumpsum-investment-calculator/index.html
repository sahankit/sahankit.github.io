About the lumpsum calculator

Calculate future value of one-time investment with expected return. Free India lumpsum SIP alternative.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the lumpsum calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Monto de la inversión ($), Rentabilidad anual esperada (%) y Años.
  2. Get the result. Press Calcular FV. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
