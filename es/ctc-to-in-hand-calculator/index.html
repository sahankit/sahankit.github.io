How to use the CTC to in-hand

  1. Enter your details. Fill in CTC anual ($) y Deducciones totales (% del CTC).
  2. Get the result. Press Estimar. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
