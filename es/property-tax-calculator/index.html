About the property tax

Estimate annual property tax from assessed value and local rate percent. Rates vary by municipality.

Funciona en el navegador—sin registro ni instalación.

How to use the property tax

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Assessed value (₹) and Annual tax rate (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copia o descarga el resultado. No se guarda nada—recarga para empezar de nuevo.
