About the blood alcohol (BAC)

Rough blood alcohol estimate from drinks, weight, and time. Not for driving decisions.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the blood alcohol (BAC)

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Bebidas estándar, Peso (kg), sexo y Horas desde el primer trago.
  2. Get the result. Press Estimar BAC. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
