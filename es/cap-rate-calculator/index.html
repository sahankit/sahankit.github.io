About the cap rate

Calculate cap rate from net operating income and property value. Free rental property metric.

Funciona en el navegador—sin registro ni instalación.

How to use the cap rate

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Net operating income ($/year) and Property value ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate cap rate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copia o descarga el resultado. No se guarda nada—recarga para empezar de nuevo.
