About the cap rate

Calculate cap rate from net operating income and property value. Free rental property metric.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the cap rate

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Ingreso operativo neto ($/año) y Valor de la propiedad ($).
  2. Get the result. Press Calcular la tasa de capitalización. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this cap rate free?

Yes. The cap rate is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
