About the salary slip

Generate a basic printable salary slip with earnings and deductions. Browser template—not payroll software.

Funciona en el navegador—sin registro ni instalación.

How to use the salary slip

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Employee name , Basic (₹) , HRA (₹) , Deductions (₹) and From month .
  2. Get the result. Press Preview slip(s) . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copia o descarga el resultado. No se guarda nada—recarga para empezar de nuevo.
