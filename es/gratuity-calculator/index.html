Gratuity formula (simplified)

Enter monthly basic + DA and completed years. For partial years, employers may round per policy and statute—confirm with HR.

  • Common formula: (15/26) × last drawn basic + DA × years of service
  • Eligibility: typically minimum 5 years under the Payment of Gratuity Act (death/disablement exceptions apply)
  • Cap: law sets a maximum gratuity amount—verify current notified limit with HR

Preguntas frecuentes

What is the 15/26 in gratuity?

It reflects 15 days’ wages per year of service expressed as a fraction of a 26-day working month in the standard formula.

Is gratuity taxable?

Statutory exemption limits apply to qualified gratuity. Tax treatment depends on employer type and amount—consult a CA.

Does resignation after 5 years qualify?

Often yes if continuous service and eligibility tests are met. Employer records prevail.

Is last drawn salary only basic?

The Act focuses on basic wages and dearness allowance—not full CTC. This tool uses basic + DA as you enter it.

¿gratuity (India) es gratis?

Sí. gratuity (India) es gratis en el navegador, sin cuenta.

¿Mis datos salen del dispositivo?

En la mayoría de las herramientas, los datos se quedan en tu dispositivo. Si una herramienta llama a una API pública (por ejemplo la edad del dominio), se indica en la página.
