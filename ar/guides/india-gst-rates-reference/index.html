India GST rates reference

استخدم حاسبة ضريبة السلع والخدمات and HSN GST rate finder for invoice math. Invoice template for print-ready layouts.

Most everyday goods fall under 5%, 12%, or 18% GST; luxury and sin goods can attract 28% or compensation cess. Exports and specified essentials may be nil-rated or exempt—always match HSN/SAC on the invoice.

Standard rate bands (indicative)

  • 0% / exempt: unbranded atta, unbranded rice, fresh milk, books (specified), healthcare services (rules vary)
  • 5%: packaged food staples, economy transport, some medicines
  • 12%: processed foods, business-class air travel (examples)
  • 18%: most services, electronics, soaps, IT services (SAC)
  • 28% + cess: luxury cars, aerated drinks, tobacco (compensation cess may apply)

Composition & reverse charge (remember)

Composition dealers pay tax at a fixed turnover percentage and generally cannot collect GST on invoices. Reverse charge applies when registered persons buy from unregistered suppliers in notified cases—use the GST calculator and your CA for filing decisions.

الأسئلة الشائعة

Is this an official GST rate list?

No. It is a simplified reference for common categories. Use the GST portal or notifications for compliance.

How do I find GST for my HSN code?

Use the HSN GST rate finder tool and cross-check the CBIC rate schedule for your exact code.
