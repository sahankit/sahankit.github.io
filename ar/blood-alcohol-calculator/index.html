About the blood alcohol (BAC)

Rough blood alcohol estimate from drinks, weight, and time. Not for driving decisions.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the blood alcohol (BAC)

  1. Enter your details. Fill in المشروبات القياسية, الوزن (كجم), الجنس و ساعات منذ أول مشروب.
  2. Get the result. Press تقدير BAC. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this blood alcohol (BAC) free?

Yes. The blood alcohol (BAC) is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
