DOCX to PDF locally

Upload a .docx file. We convert it to HTML with mammoth, then render a PDF with html2pdf.js—all on your device.

Legacy .doc files are not supported; open them in Word and Save As .docx first.

How to use the word to PDF

  1. Enter your details. Choose a file from your device. It is processed locally and never uploaded to this site.
  2. Get the result. Press Convert to PDF . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. انسخ النتيجة أو نزّلها. لا يُحفظ شيء—حدّث الصفحة للبدء من جديد.

When to use the word to PDF

Turn a Word .docx file into a PDF in your browser. Private conversion with mammoth and html2pdf—no cloud upload. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
