  1. Enter your details. Fill in رقمية (على سبيل المثال 755) و رمزي.
  2. Get the result. Press رقم → روكس. Results update on the page.

Frequently asked questions

Is this chmod calculator free?

Yes. The chmod calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
