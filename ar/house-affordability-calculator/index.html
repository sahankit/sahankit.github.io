How to use the house affordability

  1. Enter your details. Fill in إجمالي الدخل الشهري ($), الحد الأقصى لدفعة السكن (% من الدخل), الدفعة الأولى ($), معدل الرهن العقاري (٪ أبريل) و مدة القرض (سنوات).
  2. Get the result. Press تقدير. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this house affordability free?

Yes. The house affordability is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
