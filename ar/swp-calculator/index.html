How to use the SWP calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Starting corpus (₹) , Monthly withdrawal (₹) and Expected annual return (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate duration . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. انسخ النتيجة أو نزّلها. لا يُحفظ شيء—حدّث الصفحة للبدء من جديد.
