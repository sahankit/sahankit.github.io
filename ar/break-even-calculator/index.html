How to use the break-even

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Fixed costs , Price per unit and Variable cost per unit .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. انسخ النتيجة أو نزّلها. لا يُحفظ شيء—حدّث الصفحة للبدء من جديد.

When to use the break-even

Find break-even units from fixed costs, price, and variable cost per unit. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.
