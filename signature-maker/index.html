Draw or type your signature

Draw with a mouse, trackpad, stylus or your finger on a phone—strokes are smoothed so they look natural. Use Undo to remove the last stroke. Type your name and pick from six handwriting fonts for a neat typed signature.

Download a transparent PNG to place over documents in Word, Google Docs or PDF editors, or a JPG with a white background for forms that require JPG. The image is trimmed to your signature and saved at high resolution.

Using your signature on forms

  • Exam and job portals often want a JPG signature of 10–20 KB—use the Reduce image size tool after downloading.
  • Blue or black ink is accepted almost everywhere.
  • An image of a signature is not the same as a certificate-based digital signature (DSC) used for official e-filing.

How to use the signature maker

Frequently asked questions

Is the signature saved or uploaded?

No. It exists only in your browser until you download it.

Is an image signature legally valid?

Many organisations accept scanned or drawn signatures for everyday documents, but legal requirements vary. Check with the receiving party for contracts and official filings.

Why does the typed font look plain at first?

Handwriting fonts load from Google Fonts the first time you open Type mode; give it a second on slow connections.

Is this signature maker free?

Yes. The signature maker is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
