How to use voice typing

  • Choose your language—English (India, US or UK), Hindi or another Indian or world language.
  • Press Start dictation and allow microphone access.
  • Speak naturally. Grey text shows what’s being recognised; it becomes final when you pause.
  • In English, say “comma”, “full stop”, “question mark” or “new line” to add punctuation.
  • Edit the transcript, then copy it or download it as a .txt file.

Browser support and privacy

Voice typing uses your browser’s built-in speech recognition: Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on computers and Android, and Safari on iPhone and iPad. Firefox doesn’t support it yet. In Chrome, audio is sent to Google’s speech service to be transcribed; your transcript stays in this page until you copy or download it.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work in Hindi?

Yes. Choose हिन्दी Hindi from the language list and speak; the text appears in Devanagari.

Why does it stop listening?

Browsers end recognition after silence. The tool restarts automatically while dictation is on; press Stop when you’re done.

Can I transcribe an audio file?

Not directly—this tool listens to your microphone in real time.

Is this speech to text free?

Yes. The speech to text is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
