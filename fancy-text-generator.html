How fancy text works

These are not real fonts—they are special Unicode characters that look like styled letters. Because they are characters, you can paste them anywhere that accepts text: Instagram and TikTok bios, WhatsApp messages, Discord names, X posts, YouTube comments and game nicknames.

Type once and every style updates instantly. Tap Copy next to a style and paste it where you need it.

Tips and limitations

  • Screen readers may read styled letters awkwardly—avoid them for important information.
  • Some older phones show boxes for rare characters; bold and italic styles are the most widely supported.
  • Search engines and some apps treat these characters differently from normal letters, so keep your real name in plain text too.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use these fonts on Instagram?

Yes. Copy any style and paste it into your bio, captions or comments.

Why do some letters look different in a style?

Unicode only defines some letters for certain styles, so a few use the closest available symbol.

Do numbers and symbols change?

Numbers change in bold, double-struck, sans, monospace and bubble styles. Most symbols stay as they are.

Is this fancy text generator free?

Yes. The fancy text generator is free to use in your browser with no account required.
