How BMI is calculated

Body Mass Index is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared: BMI = kg ÷ m². For pounds and inches the equivalent is 703 × lb ÷ in². Example: 70 kg at 1.70 m gives 70 ÷ 2.89 = 24.2, which is in the healthy range.

The calculator also shows the weight range that keeps you between BMI 18.5 and 24.9 for your height, so you can see how far you are from the healthy band.

BMI categories for adults (WHO)

Many Asian health bodies, including guidance used in India, treat 23+ as overweight and 25+ as obese because risk rises at lower BMI. BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat, so athletes can score high while being healthy.

  • Below 18.5 — underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 — healthy weight
  • 25 to 29.9 — overweight
  • 30 and above — obese

Frequently asked questions

Can children use this BMI tool?

No. This page uses adult cut-offs. Children and teens need age- and sex-specific BMI percentile charts.

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy. Your doctor may use a different target based on age, ethnicity, and body composition.

Is BMI accurate for muscular people?

Not very. BMI only uses height and weight, so muscular people can read as overweight. Waist size or body-fat estimates add useful context.

Is this BMI calculator free?

Yes. The BMI calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?
