What’s in the favicon package

  • favicon.ico — contains 16, 32 and 48 px icons for every browser
  • favicon-16x16.png and favicon-32x32.png — modern PNG favicons
  • apple-touch-icon.png (180 px) — iPhone and iPad home screen
  • android-chrome-192x192.png and 512x512.png — Android and installable web apps
  • site.webmanifest and the HTML tags to paste into your page

Tips for a great favicon

Favicons are tiny—often 16 pixels—so keep them simple: one or two letters, a single emoji, or a bold logo mark with strong contrast. Preview the browser-tab mockup to check it’s readable. Upload the files to your site’s root folder, add the tags to every page’s <head>, and clear your browser cache to see the new icon.

Frequently asked questions

Do I still need favicon.ico?

It’s still the most compatible option, and some tools request /favicon.ico automatically. Include both the ICO and PNG files.

Can I convert a PNG to ICO?

Yes. Choose Image, upload your PNG, then download favicon.ico.

Why doesn’t my new favicon show?

Browsers cache favicons aggressively. Hard-refresh, open the favicon URL directly, or test in a private window.

Is this favicon generator free?

Yes. The favicon generator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
