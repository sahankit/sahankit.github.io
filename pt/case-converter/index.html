When to change letter case

Convert pasted headings to title case, normalize shouty caps lock to lowercase, or prepare constants in UPPERCASE. Title case capitalizes the first letter of each word—a simple rule, not full Chicago Manual style.

How to use the case converter

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Texto.
  2. Get the result. Press MAIÚSCULAS. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. The case converter is free to use in your browser with no account required.
