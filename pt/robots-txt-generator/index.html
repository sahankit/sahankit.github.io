How to use the robots.txt generator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Site URL and Disallow paths (one per line, optional) .
  2. Get the result. Press Generate . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.

When to use the robots.txt generator

Build a basic robots.txt with allow/disallow and sitemap URL. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
