About the leave encashment

Rough leave encashment from basic salary and unused leave days. Illustration for salary planning—not payroll advice.

Funciona no navegador—sem cadastro nem instalação.

How to use the leave encashment

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Monthly basic (₹) and Unused leave days .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
