What each number does

Horizontal and vertical offsets move the shadow. Blur softens the edge. Spread grows or shrinks it before the blur. Opacity is the alpha of the colour. Inset paints the shadow inside the box instead of behind it.

How to use the box shadow generator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Horizontal offset (px) , Vertical offset (px) , Blur (px) , Spread (px) and Colour .
  2. Get the result. The result updates on the page as you type.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
