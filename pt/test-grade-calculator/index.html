About the test grade calculator

Find the final exam score you need to reach a target course grade when the final has a fixed weight.

Funciona no navegador—sem cadastro nem instalação.

How to use the test grade calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Current course average (%) , Final exam weight (% of course) and Target course grade (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
