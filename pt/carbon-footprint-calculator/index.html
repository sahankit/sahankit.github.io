About the carbon footprint

Rough CO₂ from km driven using a simple kg/km factor. Educational estimate—not certified carbon audit.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the carbon footprint

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Distância por mês (km) e kg CO₂ por km.
  2. Get the result. Press Estimativa. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this carbon footprint free?

Yes. The carbon footprint is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
