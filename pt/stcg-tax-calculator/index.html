About the STCG tax (India)

Rough STCG tax on listed equity (20% on gain illustration). Not tax advice—rules vary by asset and holding period.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the STCG tax (India)

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Compra ($) e Venda ($).
  2. Get the result. Press Estimativa. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this STCG tax (India) free?

Yes. The STCG tax (India) is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
