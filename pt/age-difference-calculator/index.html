How months and days are counted

Whole years come first, then whole months, then the remaining days. If the day of the later date is earlier in the month, a month is borrowed. The total days is the straight count from the earlier date to the later one.

How to use the age difference calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Primeiro encontro e Segundo encontro.
  2. Get the result. Press Encontre a diferença. Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

A ordem das datas importa?

Não. A data anterior é tratada como o início, portanto a diferença nunca é negativa.

Is this age difference calculator free?

Yes. The age difference calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
