How to use the cost of living calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Salary in city A ($) , Cost of living index — city A and Cost of living index — city B .
  2. Get the result. Press Compare . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
