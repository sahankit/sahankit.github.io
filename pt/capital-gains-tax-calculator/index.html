About the capital gains tax calculator

Estimate US federal tax on short-term and long-term capital gains for a single filer with other ordinary income.

Funciona no navegador—sem cadastro nem instalação.

How to use the capital gains tax calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Ordinary taxable income ($) , Short-term gain ($) and Long-term gain ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate tax . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
