How to use the NPS calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Promoters (9–10) , Passives (7–8) and Detractors (0–6) .
  2. Get the result. Press NPS . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.

When to use the NPS calculator

Compute NPS from counts of promoters, passives, and detractors. Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
