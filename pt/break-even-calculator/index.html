How to use the break-even

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Custos fixos, Preço por unidade e Custo variável por unidade.
  2. Get the result. Press Calcular. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this break-even free?

Yes. The break-even is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
