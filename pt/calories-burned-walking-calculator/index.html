About the walking calories

Estimate calories burned walking from weight, distance, or steps. Illustration using MET estimate.

How to use the walking calories

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Body weight (kg) and Distance (miles) .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
