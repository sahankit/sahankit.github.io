How to use the exchange rate

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Amount and 1 unit A = how many B? .
  2. Get the result. Press Convert . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.

When to use the exchange rate

Convert amounts using your own exchange rate (no live forex API). Use it when you need a fast answer without installing software or creating an account.

Everything runs locally in your browser where the tool supports it—useful for forms, invoices, study, and quick checks on the go.
