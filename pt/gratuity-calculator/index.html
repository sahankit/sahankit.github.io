How to use the gratuity (India)

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Last drawn basic + DA (₹ monthly) and Years of service .
  2. Get the result. Press Estimate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
