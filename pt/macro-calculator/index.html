About the macro calculator

Split daily calories into protein, carbohydrates, and fat grams. Free macro calculator for meal planning.

Funciona no navegador—sem cadastro nem instalação.

How to use the macro calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Daily calories , Protein % , Carbs % and Fat % .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate macros . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
