How to use the margin calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Custo e Preço de venda.
  2. Get the result. Press Calcular margem. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

Is this margin calculator free?

Yes. The margin calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
