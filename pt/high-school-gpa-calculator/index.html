About the high school GPA

Compute unweighted high school GPA from class grades. Optional +0.5 for honors/AP per course.

Funciona no navegador—sem cadastro nem instalação.

How to use the high school GPA

  1. Enter your details. Compute unweighted high school GPA from class grades. Optional +0.5 for honors/AP per course.
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate GPA . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
