About the time card calculator

Calculate total hours worked from clock-in and clock-out times, minus break minutes.

Funciona no navegador—sem cadastro nem instalação.

How to use the time card calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Clock in , Clock out and Break (minutes) .
  2. Get the result. Press Hours worked . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
