About the work hours

Calculate paid work hours from start time, end time, and break minutes. Free timesheet helper.

How to use the work hours

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Start , End and Break (minutes) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
