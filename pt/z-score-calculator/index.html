How to use the Z-score

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Value (x) , Mean (μ) and Standard deviation (σ) .
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate z . Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
