About the reverse GST calculator

Split a GST-inclusive price into taxable value and GST amount for rates 5%, 12%, 18% or 28%.

Funciona no navegador—sem cadastro nem instalação.

How to use the reverse GST calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in GST-inclusive amount (₹) and GST rate (%) .
  2. Get the result. Press Split amount . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copie ou baixe o resultado. Nada é salvo—atualize para recomeçar.
