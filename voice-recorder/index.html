    Record in three clicks

    Press Start recording, allow microphone access and speak—the level bar shows your input volume. Pause and resume as often as you like, then press Stop. Each recording appears in a list where you can play it back, download it, or delete it.

    Recordings are saved in your browser’s native format (WebM/Opus in Chrome, Edge and Firefox; M4A in Safari). Choose Download WAV for an uncompressed file that opens in any audio editor or media player.

    Tips for clear recordings

    • Pick a headset or USB microphone from the list for better sound than a laptop mic.
    • Keep the level bar mostly in the green; red means too loud.
    • Record in a quiet room with soft furnishings to reduce echo.

    How to use the voice recorder

    1. Enter your details. Fill in Microphone.
    2. Get the result. Press Start recording. Results update on the page.
    3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a time limit?

    No fixed limit. Long recordings use more memory, so download them before closing the tab.

    Can I download MP3?

    Browsers can’t encode MP3 natively. Download WAV and convert it if you need MP3.

    Are recordings uploaded?

    No. They stay in this browser tab and are discarded when you close it.

    Is this voice recorder free?

    Yes. The voice recorder is free to use in your browser with no account required.

    Does my data leave my device?

    For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
