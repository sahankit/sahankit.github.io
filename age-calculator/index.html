Exact age from date of birth

Enter your date of birth to see your exact age in years, months and days. Change the second date to find your age on any day—useful for exam and job eligibility cut-offs (for example “age as on 1 August”), insurance forms and retirement planning.

The calculator counts calendar months correctly, including leap years and months with 28, 30 or 31 days, and also shows your total months, weeks and days lived.

How age is counted

Full years are counted first, then remaining whole months, then leftover days. If today’s day-of-month is earlier than your birth day, the month is not yet complete and days are borrowed from the previous month. People born on 29 February celebrate on 1 March in non-leap years in this calculator.

How to use the age calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Date of birth and Age on this date.
  2. Get the result. Press Calculate age. Results update on the page and recalculate as you edit values.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate age as on a specific date?

Set the second field (“Age on this date”) to the cut-off date, such as an exam eligibility date. The result updates immediately.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. The calculation uses real calendar month lengths, including 29 February.

Is this valid for legal age proof?

No. It is a calculator. Official documents such as a birth certificate are needed for legal proof of age.

Is this age calculator free?

Yes. The age calculator is free to use in your browser with no account required.

Does my data leave my device?

For most tools on this site, input stays on your device. If a tool calls a public API (for example domain age), that is noted on the page.
