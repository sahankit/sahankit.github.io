About the 401(k) calculator

Project a 401(k) balance from monthly contributions, employer match, years to retire, and expected annual return.

Runs in your browser—no signup and no install.

How to use the 401(k) calculator

  1. Enter your details. Fill in Your monthly contribution ($) , Employer match (% of your contribution) , Years until retirement , Expected annual return (%) and Current balance ($) .
  2. Get the result. Press Project balance . Results update on the page.
  3. Use or copy it. Copy or download the output. Nothing is stored—refresh the page to start over.
