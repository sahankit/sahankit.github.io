Frequently asked questions

Do these games need a download?

No. Everything runs as JavaScript in your browser tab.

Do games save progress?

Most scores reset when you refresh unless the tool says otherwise. Nothing is stored on our servers.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. Touch controls are built into snake, 2048, pong, flappy bird, and several other titles.
