Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y. Example: 15% of 2,400 = 360.
  • X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100. Example: 45 out of 60 marks = 75%.
  • Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Example: 800 → 1,000 is a 25% increase.
  • Add a percentage = value × (1 + p ÷ 100). Example: 1,200 + 18% GST = 1,416.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percentage of marks?

Divide marks obtained by total marks and multiply by 100. Use the second calculator: enter your marks and the total.

Is percentage increase the same as percentage points?

No. Going from 10% to 12% is a 2 percentage-point rise but a 20% increase relative to the starting value.

How is the result rounded?

Results show up to four decimal places. Round currency to two decimals when you use it on an invoice.
